# Enter focused patronage at tier two

Status: accepted

Imperial and Nord broad pantheon rewards own the pre-commitment tier from 25 to
49 and their completed broad tier at 50. Accepting an eligible patron at deity
piety 50 therefore suppresses the broad boon and enters that deity's focused
family at T2; focused T1 records remain save-compatible artifacts but are never
granted. Commitment and deity piety are preserved, so a fall below 50 suspends
the focused boon without cancelling the patron and recovery restores T2. T3
begins at 85. This makes commitment a change in devotional relationship rather
than a progression tax or a temporary loss of earned standing.
