# Migrate progression without renaming save identifiers

Status: accepted

The pacing and broad-pantheon transition is versioned and idempotent: old Imperial and Old Ways counts seed their pools proportionally, Nord Nine Divines seeds from the highest eligible deity, and Argonian cultural practice seeds from the old clamped composite while its relation ledgers remain intact. Legacy EditorIDs, script/property names, and StorageUtil keys may retain compatibility vocabulary, but all player-visible names are replaced, because save continuity is more important than internal naming purity and does not require exposing stale terms.
