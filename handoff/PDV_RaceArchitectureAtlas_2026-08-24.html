How to read a page

Four lanes per race, left to right in time. Spine is the progression walk. Substrate is the always-on culture layer (three races run it inline without a metric). Curse shows how vampirism and lycanthropy land for that race. The Daedric progression is identical for all ten races, so it is drawn once below; each race page carries only its Daedric flavor.

Built — verified in live source, evidence cited
Partial — built, with a verified defect or gap
Designed only — in the design docs, absent from source
Absent — nothing found in design or source
Vocabulary. Chips carry the real moment IDs (tier.reach, focus.emergence, dawn.digest…). Piety thresholds: Seeker 25 · Devoted 50 · Champion 85. Substrate metric 0–75, tiers at 1/25/75, one shared 4.0/day credit budget.
Two findings that frame every page. The Marked surfacing rung (arch 10.6: "moments the player should remember") has no implementation for any race — all contextual favor exits one generic toast call. And the coverage map's rule that the Devoted crossing surfaces via emergence is design intent only: Khajiit is the sole race with a real emergence beat; the only other emergence.onset emitter (Breton) is provably unreachable, and every other race rides the generic tier lane.

The Daedric track universal — all 16 Princes, every race

Access is universal; race enters as flavor (legibility state, stigma and exit-difficulty multipliers per Prince). Pacts are exclusive among themselves. Committing a Divine patron severs the pact; taking a pact does not sever the patron.

What the matrix says. The spine and substrates are solidly built everywhere they're meant to exist. The unevenness is concentrated in the announcement layer: champion entry is a Kyne-only feature shown through the standard path, emergence is Khajiit-only, and five races handle curses correctly while telling the player nothing. Two dead state keys (Orc and Redguard VampireScar) and the Dunmer no-decay question are the loose bolts. This matches the tranche's running theme: the mod computes state transitions reliably and announces them inconsistently.
