# Devotional Medallions — brief for the graphic artist

Concept reference pack for the **devotional medallion** — the in-world pendant a character wears for the
god or Daedric Prince they worship. These sheets establish a consistent design language and show every
worship target on its medallion. They're concepts to riff on, not finished art.

## Design language
- **Form:** a round pendant amulet, ~40mm, on a suspension **bail**.
- **Structure:** a **raised polished-metal bezel** (with a fine beaded rim on most), a **recessed field**
  (matte/patina, darker), and the **deity glyph embossed proud** so it catches light.
- **The glyph is the constant; the material is the variable.** Same silhouette, different metal/finish per
  pantheon — the material tells the player which tradition the medallion belongs to.

## Materials by pantheon
Divines = warm gold · Daedric = black ebony/obsidian with a cold or blood-red glyph · Khajiit = silver /
moonstone · Yokudan = desert bronze · Altmer = pale pearl-gold · Nord = weathered iron · Bosmer =
livewood/amber · Argonian = bone-jade · Concept/substrate marks = open (pick with the mode).

## Scale & legibility (important)
These render in-game at small sizes (inventory icon and a ~20–40px HUD mark), so the **glyph must read at a
glance**. Keep the central silhouette bold; treat the bezel/material as supporting. The flat line-glyph
versions (below) are the fallback the UI uses at the smallest sizes.

## Source art (vectors you can pull the glyphs from)
The exact glyph line-art (SVG path data) lives in:
- `handoff/PrismaGlyph_FullRoster_SVGData.md` — Princes, cultural pantheons, concept marks
- `handoff/PrismaGlyph_Substrate_SVGData.md` — Hist/ancestor/lunar/etc.
- `handoff/PrismaGlyph_Tier0_SVGData.md` — Y'ffre / Z'en / Baan Dar
- base Divines glyphs are in `native/.../PrismaUI/views/Devotion/app.js` (`symbolSpecs`)
The sheets themselves are generated by `scratch/prisma-art/medallions.py` + `med_render.py` (Python +
cairosvg) — editable if you want different framings/material combos rendered.

## What's most useful back
Whatever suits your pipeline — refined medallion icons, an inventory/3D model treatment, or just sharper
takes on a few flagship pieces (Kyne, Hircine, the Khajiit moons are good showcases). A few marks are still
first-pass; happy to adjust any glyph silhouette before you commit time to it.
