# Prisma glyph roster — refinement flags & lore dive

Status after the lore-grounded second pass (`scratch/prisma-art/batch6_refined.png`). The 19 flagged marks
were re-researched against the mod's own lore (`references/skyrim-deity-reference.jsx`) and canonical TES
iconography, then redesigned. Drop-in data (refined): `PrismaGlyph_FullRoster_SVGData.md`.

## Solar-mark collision — resolved
Only `meridia` stays a rayed sun (canonically "the Sunburst is her symbol"). `molag-bal`, `namira`,
`broad` moved off the sun; `magnus` keeps a sun **with** an architect's compass (distinct enough);
`trinimac`'s sun is a crown element, not the whole mark.

## Still worth a glance (acceptable, not perfect)
- `mehrunes-dagon` — improved, but the multi-blade can still read busy at 24px; a single ornate razor is a fallback.
- `tsun` — the battleaxe reads slightly tulip/anchor-ish at 24px.
- `satakal` — the bite point is legible but tight.
