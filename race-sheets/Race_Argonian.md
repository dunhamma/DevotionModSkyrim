# Argonian

> The Hist is far away. The people are here. The void is always close. You begin inside absence, not abundance.

## Who They Worship

Argonians don't have religion in the way other races understand it. There are no gods to choose between. No temples to attend. No prayers that invoke a name from a pantheon.

What Argonians have is the **Hist** — the ancient sentient trees of Black Marsh that give Argonian souls at birth and receive them back at death. The Hist isn't worshipped. It's *constitutive*. An Argonian without the Hist isn't a faithless person — they're an incomplete one.

In Skyrim, there are no Hist trees. The connection that defines Saxhleel identity is stretched thin across thousands of miles. What remains is maintenance, memory, and community — the slow work of holding yourself together when the source of your selfhood is unreachably distant.

## How Devotion Works for Argonians

**Worship type:** Layered Custom (Mono) — not a pantheon, not a choice. A structure of identity that must be maintained against decay.

**No setup choice.** Argonians begin inside their layered exile system automatically. The player begins inside *absence*, not abundance.

**Three layers, not three equal gods:**

| Layer | What it answers | Weight |
|-------|-----------------|--------|
| **Hist relation** | How connected am I to what makes me Saxhleel at all? | Primary (always) |
| **Collective / community identity** | If the Hist is distant, am I still held together by my people? | Secondary (survival structure) |
| **Sithis acknowledgment** | Am I making meaning through change, death, and void rather than through belonging? | Tertiary (rises under pressure) |

**The hierarchy matters.** These aren't three equal devotion pools. The Hist is constitutive. Community buffers its absence. Sithis rises only when the other two weaken. A healthy Argonian is Hist-connected with strong community. A grieving one finds Sithis growing louder.

**Bed-of-choice:** Argonians designate one bed or home location as "the family I chose." Sleeping there regularly maintains community identity and slows exile decay. Any town can work once chosen. Windhelm gets extra flavor because the Assemblage matters, but it is not the only valid mechanical home.

## The Hist Relation

The always-primary layer. Harder to build than community. Decays without maintenance.

**What maintains it:**
- Water, swamp, and wetland proximity — being near the element that connects all Hist
- Rest and reflection in solitude — meditative stillness, not distraction
- Waterway and riverside moments — the world's echo of Black Marsh
- Hist sap meditation — a custom item/power used for deliberate reconnection when vanilla Skyrim has no real Hist infrastructure

**What doesn't help:**
- Generic combat victories
- Ordinary quest completion
- Social activity (that feeds community, not Hist)
- Being busy, productive, or heroic

**The decay:** Without maintenance, Hist relation decays slowly but constantly. This is intentional — it reflects the real experience of exile. You can't just *not lose it*. You have to actively maintain it. The Hist is far away. Remembering takes work.

**Design honesty:** The mod doesn't pretend Skyrim has rich Hist infrastructure. The maintenance signals are environmental and reflective — honest about what Skyrim can detect. Custom Hist sap meditation is expected because the layer needs at least one active ritual tool to feel playable.

## Collective / Community Identity

The main exile survival structure. Stronger vanilla-facing hooks than the Hist layer.

**What builds it:**
- Helping Argonians anywhere in Skyrim (standard weight)
- Supporting Windhelm's Assemblage (extra-heavy weight)
- Protecting marginalized Saxhleel and preserving identity under pressure
- Community solidarity acts of any kind

**What it does:**
- Partially buffers low Hist relation (community holds you when the trees can't)
- Provides its own devotion path (community-facing blessings and privileges)
- Cannot *replace* the Hist, but can make its absence survivable

**Windhelm weight:** The Assemblage and Windhelm's Argonian quarter carry the heaviest community weight in the game. This is the most concentrated Argonian community in Skyrim. Supporting it matters more than scattered help elsewhere.

Arkay priest reactions are also considered essential custom content for the full Argonian release. Argonian death rites and Hist-soul beliefs need the world to answer back; otherwise the theology stays trapped in internal counters.

## Sithis Acknowledgment

Every Argonian has a baseline awareness of Sithis — the void, change, death-facing acceptance. But this isn't worship. It's acknowledgment. Recognition that the void is part of reality.

**How it grows:**
- Dark Brotherhood membership and quest stages (strongest active signal)
- Explicit death-facing or void-acknowledging quest choices
- *Not* from generic stealth or generic killing

**What it doesn't do:**
- Replace the Hist
- Fully compensate for Hist loss
- Become "good" just because it grows

**The Skyrim exile rule:** Sithis is more foregrounded in exile conditions than in homeland Argonian life. When the Hist is distant and community is thin, the void becomes louder. This is honest to the lore — but it doesn't promote Sithis to equal status with the Hist.

**A Sithis-heavy Argonian is not a healthy Argonian.** It's a stable one, possibly — but stability through void-acceptance is not the same as wholeness through Hist connection.

## The Daedric Question

Argonians don't engage with the Daedric/Aedric framework at all. Their cosmology is entirely separate:

- **Hist** — Native. The only constitutive source.
- **Sithis** — Native. Acknowledged void/change. Not the same as Dark Brotherhood's reading, but connected.
- **Everything else** — Foreign. The Aedra are someone else's gods. The Daedra are someone else's temptations. None of them have theological weight for Argonians.

This means Daedric quests don't create the theological tension they do for other races. An Argonian completing Azura's Star isn't committing apostasy — they're just... doing a task. The spiritual damage, if any, comes from what *else* they're neglecting while doing it.

- **Molag Bal (vampirism)** — Not taboo in the Daedric sense, but devastating because of what it does to Hist relation.
- **Hircine (werewolfism)** — Strains Hist but doesn't collapse it.
- **Dark Brotherhood (Sithis)** — Actually *native-adjacent*. The strongest active Sithis lane.

## Curse States

**Vampire:**
One of the deepest grief states in the entire mod. The Hist is hit hardest — may collapse into silence or corruption. Community is also damaged (the undead don't belong to the living). Sithis acknowledgment becomes more available or foregrounded, but not automatically *good* — it's the void growing louder because everything else has gone quiet.

This is not "vampire Argonian finds a dark path." This is "vampire Argonian loses the thing that made them themselves." Recovery (if cured) is slow, painful, and scarred.

**Werewolf:**
Less devastating than vampirism. Strains Hist relation but doesn't collapse it. Community damage is mild compared to vampire. Sithis remains mostly neutral. The beast is a *strain* on identity, not a replacement of it.

The comparison: vampirism for an Argonian attacks their *soul source*. Werewolfism attacks their *shape*. Since Argonian identity runs deeper than shape, the werewolf landing is survivable in ways the vampire landing is not.

## Playing This Race — What to Expect

Playing an Argonian in Devotion feels like **exile, distance, and the slow work of holding yourself together**.

You begin in absence. The Hist is far away. Your devotion system doesn't hand you options and ask you to pick — it presents you with a fragile connection and asks you to maintain it. Water, stillness, and solitude feed the Hist relation. Community feeds your belonging. Sithis grows in the spaces between.

The distinctive Argonian experience is *maintenance against decay*. Where other races build toward something, you're fighting to keep what you already have from slipping away. The Hist decay isn't a punishment — it's the honest expression of what exile means. The work of restoration is the worship.

If you're the kind of player who gravitates toward the Dark Brotherhood, the Argonian system makes that choice *theologically real* rather than just mechanically rewarding. Sithis rising doesn't mean you're becoming evil — it means the void is becoming your primary framework for meaning because the Hist can't reach you anymore.

And if you maintain your Hist connection carefully — seeking water, taking time in solitude, meditating rather than raging — you get to feel what it means to carry your soul-source across an impossible distance. The blessings are quieter than other races. But they mean more.
