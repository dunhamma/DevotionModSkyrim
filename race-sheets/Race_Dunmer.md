# Dunmer

**Status:** Player-facing companion sheet. Implementation authority lives in `PDV_RaceDesign_Dunmer.md`, `PDV_TargetEndStates_1.0.md`, and `references/PDV_RaceArchitecture_DesignReference.md`; launch content weighting remains tunable.

> The ancestors are always watching. The question is not whether you pray — it is whether your prayer carries weight across the ash.

## Who They Worship

Dunmer religion is layered, not competitive. At its foundation is **ancestor worship** — the daily obligation to maintain connection with the dead through ash-prayer, shrine maintenance, and offering. On top of this foundation sit the **three Good Daedra** — Azura, Boethiah, and Mephala — who are not outsider demons as other races might see them, but recognized protectors re-emerging as the dominant faith after the Tribunal's fall.

In 4E 201, a Dunmer in Skyrim carries this entire structure in exile. There are no ancestral tombs to visit. No House shrines to maintain. The daily practice continues anyway — a portable faith carried across the ash, sustained by will alone.

## How Devotion Works for Dunmer

**Worship type:** Semi-Mono — layered and cumulative. Not competing paths, but reinforcing depths.

**No setup choice needed.** Dunmer worship begins automatically in its layered structure:

- **Layer 1 (always active):** Ancestor ash-prayer. This never turns off. Every Dunmer maintains this regardless of everything else.
- **Layer 2 (standard for devout):** Good Daedra acknowledgment. Azura, Boethiah, and Mephala alongside ancestor practice. Natural deepening, not a separate choice.
- **Layer 3 (optional depth):** Primary Good Daedra commitment. Choose *one* of the three as your focused patron. Unlocks Devoted tier.

**The critical distinction:** Choosing a primary Good Daedra never competes with ancestor devotion. They are theologically the same tradition at different depths. Committing to Azura doesn't reduce your ash-prayer — it adds weight on top.

**Broad worship** (Layers 1-2 without primary commitment) can reach **Faithful**. **Devoted** requires focusing on one of the three Good Daedra.

**Portable shrine practice:** Dunmer carry their ancestor infrastructure with them. Prayer is centered on a permanent portable shrine item with a triggered ceremony, so exile does not require pretending Skyrim has ancestral tombs everywhere. Praying in a player-owned home gives a bonus because the house becomes your local ancestor-space; no specific home is privileged over another.

## Paths of Devotion

### The Ancestor Layer — Always Active
*The foundation everything else sits on.*

- **What you do:** Maintain daily practice — morning and evening shrine acknowledgment, reflective acts, community solidarity. Support Grey Quarter Dunmer (solidarity in exile counts). Fulfill burial and family duties when Skyrim exposes them.
- **What you get:** The baseline blessing of maintained connection. Modest but steady — the ancestors are present and they see you.
- **The feel:** A Dunmer carrying their entire spiritual infrastructure in exile, refusing to let distance make them forget.

### Azura — The Twilight Lady
*For those drawn to fate, prophecy, and the threshold between states.*

- **What you do:** Complete Azura's Star quest. Honor dawn and dusk — the liminal hours. Accept prophetic moments. Cross thresholds deliberately.
- **What you get:** Contextual favors tied to twilight, transition, and foresight — strongest at the boundaries between things.
- **The feel:** A Dunmer who listens to fate's whisper and acts when the moment is right.

### Boethiah — The Prince of Plots
*For those who prove strength beyond mere survival.*

- **What you do:** Complete Boethiah's Calling. Win rivalries. Overthrow those who stand above you through strength and will, not treachery alone.
- **What you get:** Blessings tied to conflict momentum — the edge you gain from proving yourself against real opposition.
- **The feel:** A Dunmer who believes power must be *taken*, not given, and that proving is an act of devotion.

### Mephala — The Webspinner
*For those who keep secrets and protect hidden communities.*

- **What you do:** Complete The Whispering Door. Maintain hidden knowledge. Protect secret networks. Choose discretion over exposure in quest resolutions.
- **What you get:** Favors tied to secrecy, hidden leverage, and the advantage of knowing what others don't.
- **The feel:** A Dunmer who understands that the web holds people together as much as it traps them.

## The Daedric Question

Dunmer have the most nuanced Daedric relationship of any race. The Good Daedra (Azura, Boethiah, Mephala) are **native** — not outsider corruption but integrated protectors. This is unique in Tamriel.

- **Azura, Boethiah, Mephala** — Native. No stigma, no penalty. Standard Dunmer deepening.
- **Malacath** — Taboo. House of Troubles-adjacent.
- **Hircine** — Foreign. No Dunmer framework for the beast-shape.
- **Molag Bal** — Taboo, but accessible under vampirism (see Curse States).
- **Hermaeus Mora** — Foreign but not uniquely catastrophic. Scholars may engage cautiously.
- **Nocturnal, Sheogorath** — Taboo. Hidden-network overlap doesn't make Nocturnal welcome; madness path is House of Troubles pressure.

The key distinction: for Dunmer, the Good Daedra are *not Daedric worship* in the way other races would understand it. They're legitimate ancestral protectors who happen to also be Daedric princes.

## Curse States

**Vampire:**
Ash-prayer goes silent. The ancestors do not respond to the undead. Layer 1 events become inert — logged but carrying no weight. But the Good Daedra path *intensifies*:
- Boethiah and Mephala remain fully reachable
- Azura remains possible (her relationship with vampires is complicated in lore)
- Molag Bal becomes available as a hostile but functional patron

On cure: Ash-prayer restores. The ancestors resume contact. But a permanent scar remains — the ancestors noticed the silence. They remember.

**Werewolf:**
The most theologically homeless Dunmer curse. Hircine has *no framework* in Dunmer cosmology — not recognized by the Reclamations, not part of the ancestor tradition.
- Ash-prayer continues but strained (half weight)
- Good Daedra tolerate it more than ancestors do (three-quarter weight)
- No alternative path opens. Unlike the vampire, there's nowhere else to go.
- Minor exception: Telvanni-associated acts reduce the penalty slightly (Telvanni tolerate unusual transformations)

The comparison: Vampire closes the primary path but opens a viable alternative. Werewolf strains everything without offering anything new. Vampire is more mechanically interesting; werewolf is more theologically isolated.

## The Infrastructure Ceiling

There's an honest design acknowledgment built into Dunmer: Skyrim doesn't contain ancestral tombs, House shrines, or proper Dunmer religious infrastructure. The mod doesn't pretend otherwise. Your ash-prayer is maintained through portable practice, but certain acts (proper burial rites, tomb visits, House acknowledgment) simply can't happen in Skyrim.

This naturally reflects diaspora reality. The ceiling isn't a punishment — it's an honest expression of what exile means.

## Tribunal Memory

All Dunmer carry a quiet awareness of the Tribunal's fall — Vivec, Sotha Sil, Almalexia. This is not a full path, but it can matter during shrine prayer. A Tribunal shrine may answer with a small random buff or debuff based on where the character's thoughts wandered. The pool is curated and thematic, reflecting the Three's unstable legacy rather than a controllable blessing machine.

The Tribunal is a dying tradition in 4E 201, and the mod treats it that way: meaningful, remembered, sometimes spiritually volatile, never replacing the Good Daedra and ancestor structure.

## Playing This Race — What to Expect

Playing a Dunmer in Devotion feels **cumulative and internally coherent**. There are no competing gods pulling you in different directions. Everything reinforces everything else. Your ancestor practice supports your Good Daedra acknowledgment, which deepens into primary commitment — each layer adding rather than replacing.

The distinctive Dunmer experience is that *you're never starting from zero*. The ancestor layer is always active, always counting. Even a Dunmer who hasn't committed to a primary patron is still maintaining daily practice. The gods and the dead are the same tradition at different depths.

The exile dimension adds weight to everything. Grey Quarter solidarity, diaspora survival, maintaining practice without infrastructure — these aren't just quest hooks, they're expressions of what it means to carry an entire religion inside yourself far from home.
