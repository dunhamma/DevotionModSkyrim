# Nord

> The gods of Skyrim do not ask for prayer. They ask for deeds.

## Who They Worship

Nords live between two overlapping pantheons — the **Old Ways** (Shor, Kyne, Tsun, Stuhn, Mara, Talos) and the **Nine Divines** (the Imperial pantheon adopted alongside their own). In practice, most Nords blend both traditions freely. A Nord might honor Kyne on the mountain and Arkay at a funeral without feeling any contradiction.

What makes Nordic worship distinctive is that it's *earned* rather than declared. The gods watch. When a Nord lives in a way that catches a god's attention, that god reaches back.

## How Devotion Works for Nords

**Worship type:** Pantheon (Poly) — multiple gods are available, blending is normal.

**Your first choice:** At character creation, you align with either the Old Ways (6 gods) or the Nine Divines (9 gods). This determines your available pantheon.

**Broad worship** is the starting state. You honor your whole pantheon through varied living. This can take you up to **Faithful** — a solid, recognized relationship with the divine. Most Nords live here.

**Focused worship** unlocks when a single god *notices you*. If your actions consistently align with one deity's domain — sustained combat excellence for Shor, outdoor living for Kyne, defiance of the Thalmor for Talos — that god will eventually make an offer. Accept, and you can reach **Devoted**. Decline, and you continue in broad worship with no penalty.

This isn't a menu. It's emergent from how you play.

## Paths of Devotion

### Shor — The Warrior-King
*For those who fight with purpose and dream of Sovngarde.*

- **What you do:** Win honorable combat against hostile foes. Complete the Companions questline. Walk Sovngarde. Face Tsun's trial.
- **What you get:** Blessings that honor the warrior's spirit — endurance, presence, and the knowledge that your seat in the Hall of Valor is being carved.
- **The feel:** A Nord who fights because fighting *matters*, not for coin or cruelty.

### Kyne — The Storm-Mother
*For those who live beneath the sky and listen to the wind.*

- **What you do:** Sleep outdoors. Live in the wilderness. Complete Greybeard milestones. Honor the natural world without domesticating it.
- **What you get:** Favors tied to weather, the wild, and the storm — strongest when you're exposed to the elements rather than sheltered behind walls.
- **The feel:** A Nord whose temple is the mountainside and whose hymn is the gale.

### Talos / Ysmir — The Defiant
*For those who refuse to let the Thalmor erase their god.*

- **What you do:** Side with the Stormcloaks. Protect Talos worshippers. Activate hidden shrines. Resist Thalmor authority through quest choices.
- **What you get:** Religious privileges tied to defiance — the more dangerous your faith, the more Talos rewards it.
- **The feel:** A Nord whose worship is an act of war.

### Tsun / Stuhn — The Honorable Trial
*For those who fight with mercy and prove themselves through struggle.*

- **What you do:** Join the Companions. Complete warrior-society milestones. Face trials with honor rather than expedience.
- **What you get:** Blessings of martial integrity — combat conduct that is watched and rewarded.
- **The feel:** A Nord who cares *how* they fight, not just that they win.

### Mara — The Hearth
*For those who build and protect rather than wander and destroy.*

- **What you do:** Marry. Build a home. Defend your hold. Restore communities. Complete quests that protect the vulnerable.
- **What you get:** Blessings tied to home, community, and the bonds that hold a people together.
- **The feel:** A Nord whose strength is measured in what they've built, not what they've broken.

### Akatosh / Kynareth / Others (Nine Divines paths)
*For those who worship through the Imperial reading of the same forces.*

- The Nine Divines versions of these gods share many triggers with their Old Ways counterparts but frame them through Imperial civic theology rather than Nordic mythic tradition.
- Zenithar rewards honest commerce. Arkay honors death rites. Julianos values wisdom. Dibella celebrates beauty and art. Stendarr asks for mercy in combat.

## The Daedric Question

For Nords, Daedric worship is **taboo** across the board. The Nordic religious frame is built on communal honor, hearth-safety, and gods who fought alongside mortal men. Daedric princes represent the opposite — alien forces that take rather than give.

- **Azura, Boethiah, Mephala, Nocturnal, Hermaeus Mora** — all severe taboo. Intelligible as temptation but corrosive to Nordic honor.
- **Hircine** — the exception-that-isn't. The Companions' curse makes Hircine *available* but never *welcome*. It's a strain on Sovngarde, not a path to it.
- **Molag Bal** — vampirism's source. The ultimate violation of Nordic afterlife theology.

Consequences of straying: loss of standing with your patron, increased difficulty recovering devotion, and a sense that the Nordic divine frame is pulling away from you.

## Curse States

**Werewolf:** Hircine pulls against Shor and Sovngarde. Your combat devotion shifts toward the hunt rather than honorable battle. The Companions questline makes this accessible, but it's a theological compromise — your afterlife claim weakens while the beast endures.

**Vampire:** Severs your claim to Sovngarde entirely. The Nordic divine frame does not accommodate undeath. Molag Bal's shadow falls across everything. Devotion to your patron collapses rather than merely slowing. Cure is possible, but the scar remains.

## Playing This Race — What to Expect

Playing a Nord in Devotion feels like *being noticed*. You don't manage a prayer schedule or click shrines on cooldown — you live, and the gods respond. Fight with honor and Shor turns his eye your way. Sleep under Kyne's sky and she breathes closer. Defy the Thalmor and Talos marks you.

The tension is that broad worship is comfortable but shallow. To reach the deepest tier, you must let one god claim you — and that means your playstyle narrows. A Nord who does everything is beloved by all gods equally, which is to say: not deeply by any one. The question is whether you're content with Faithful, or hungry for Devoted.
