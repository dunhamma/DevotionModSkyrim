# Walking Under the Moons -- A Khajiit's Guide to Devotion

The Lunar Lattice holds every Khajiit. You strengthen it by observing the moons,
sleeping beneath the open sky, remembering lunar lore, defending caravans, and
living the stories of the Khajiit gods. The first such practice in each
devotional day advances the shared lunar path.

The road itself is home. A completed, uninterrupted sleep that began outdoors
counts whether it used a bedroll or a Campfire tent. Sleeping indoors, waking
from an interrupted sleep, or reaching a sleep stop without a captured start
context does not count.

## When a road finds its name

At first you remain broad within the Lattice. When one god's pattern clearly
leads your behavior and that god has reached Seeker standing, the path names
itself. This happens automatically. The first emergence receives one ceremonial
message, a toast, and a pinned entry in the Book of Days. If another god later
earns a clear lead and Seeker standing, the path turns without another popup.
An established focus is not erased by a tie or a temporary loss of standing.

- **Khenarthi** answers open-road rest, passage, mercy, and caravan belonging.
- **Azurah** answers moon observation, thresholds, twilight, and the old lunar
  mysteries.
- **Baan Dar** answers the clever reversal, the outnumbered victory, and the
  survival of those the comfortable world rejects.
- **Rajhin** answers elegant, story-worthy theft rather than petty crime.
- **Alkosh** answers dragon-order, Words of Power, and great acts that hold chaos
  to its proper course.

## The god in strength

The sky moves through eight god-strength slots: Alkosh, Azurah, Khenarthi,
Rajhin, Rajhin, Baan Dar, Khenarthi, and Azurah. Observe the Moons names the god
currently in strength and draws one contemplation from that god's lore or from a
shared lunar pool.

When the god in strength is also your focused god, your focused numeric blessing
enters **Lattice Resonance** and becomes 20% stronger. The Active Effects menu
shows a marker while this is happening. Resonance does not strengthen the broad
lunar substrate or scripted Champion gifts.

## The three lunar rewards

- **Lunar Hardiness:** Disease Resistance +5%.
- **Khajiit Lunar Road:** Stamina +10 and Disease Resistance +10%.
- **Lunar Attunement:** Stamina +15 and Disease Resistance +15%.

Only the strongest earned lunar reward is active.

## The five focused roads

- **Khenarthi:** rising Stamina, Carry Weight, and finally Movement Speed.
- **Azurah:** rising Magicka and Magic Resistance. At Champion, Azurah's Portent
  becomes a once-daily lesser power that briefly reveals living beings, undead,
  corpses, Daedra, and Dwarven automatons through nearby walls.
- **Baan Dar:** rising Unarmed Damage and Health. At Champion, Baan Dar can once
  per devotional day pull you back to 50% Health when you fall near death.
- **Rajhin:** Sneak, Lockpicking, and finally Pickpocket.
- **Alkosh:** Armor, Block, and finally faster Shout recovery.

Focused rewards are highest-tier-only: Seeker, Devoted, and Champion packages
replace one another rather than stacking.

## When the dark gets in

Lycanthropy strains the Lattice; vampirism corrupts it; sustained shadow-coded
predation can create Shadow Drift. These pressures damage belonging without
erasing Khajiit identity. Ordinary night travel, honest stealth, and moon
observation do not cause Shadow Drift.

Walk the road. Watch the moons. Let the life you live reveal which god has been
walking beside you.
