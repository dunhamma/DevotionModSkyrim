# PDV Nord Lore Baseline

**Status: LIVING**
**Date:** 2026-08-27
**Purpose:** Sourced Nord lore baseline for the Wave 1 roster decision (the offer-list vs
family-list reconciliation) and future copy work. Lore claims cite the in-game text they rest
on. Claims that rest only on out-of-game material (developer texts, printed guides, forum
posts) are flagged as such. This document records evidence and options; it decides nothing.
Owner rulings land in the final section.

**Source-tier legend:**
- IN-GAME: readable book or spoken dialogue inside a shipped TES game (Morrowind, Oblivion,
  Skyrim, ESO). Era tag on every citation: 4E 201 is the mod's present; ESO is 2E (~700 years
  earlier); Morrowind/Oblivion books are 3E copies of older texts.
- FLAVOR: in-game item/antiquity/codex description text (canon but thin).
- OOG: official out-of-game material (Prima guide, Shor Son of Shor, The Five Hundred Mighty
  Companions, developer posts). Flagged wherever load-bearing.

The backbone source is Varieties of Faith in the Empire (Brother Mikhael Karkuxor), whose
Skyrim pantheon list and per-god entries carry most of section 2. Crucially for era
discipline, this book SHIPS IN 4E SKYRIM (added by Dragonborn), so its claims are readable
in-world at the mod's own date. ESO ships a near-identical Nord excerpt, Varieties of Faith:
The Nords (2E), adding section headings ("The Eight", "Testing Gods", "Dead God") the
classic lacks. Standing hazard: much vivid Nordic-pantheon material is Kirkbride-adjacent --
Five Songs of King Wulfharth and The Arcturian Heresy are IN-GAME (Morrowind, 3E) but framed
in-world as heretical/legendary; Shor Son of Shor and The Five Hundred Mighty Companions are
OOG and NOT used as evidence below.

## 1. State of the faith in 4E 201

**The live wound is Talos.** The White-Gold Concordat (4E 175) outlawed Talos worship
empire-wide (The Great War, Skyrim 4E book; The Talos Mistake, Skyrim 4E -- the Imperial
apologia, useful for the loyalist voice). Enforcement was loose until the Markarth Incident:
Ulfric retook Markarth for the promise of free Talos worship, the Thalmor invoked the
treaty, and Justiciars gained the run of Skyrim (The Great War; The Bear of Markarth, Skyrim
4E, Imperial-sympathetic). By 4E 201 the civil war is explicitly religious in Nord
self-description: Stormcloak oaths invoke Talos, Justiciars execute roadside worshippers,
and Northwatch Keep holds Nords taken for their faith (IN-GAME: Skyrim events; quest Missing
in Action). Every Nord the player meets has already taken a position on this.

**Two religious languages coexist without a schism.** Skyrim's public temple infrastructure at
4E 201 is Imperialized: the Temple of the Divines in Solitude keeps eight shrines and one
EMPTY ALCOVE where Talos stood (IN-GAME: Skyrim, Temple of the Divines interior; Freir and
Rorlund dialogue); Kynareth's temple in Whiterun (Danica Pure-Spring), Mara's in Riften
(Maramal), Dibella's in Markarth (Hamal), and Arkay's Halls of the Dead in the major holds
(Andurs, Helgird, Runil) are all live 4E practice, largely staffed by Nords. Meanwhile the
old names persist as living speech and conviction rather than institution: "Shor's bones" and
"By Ysmir" are everyday oaths, guards and Companions talk of Sovngarde as a real destination,
and Windhelm keeps an open Temple of Talos under Ulfric (IN-GAME: Skyrim generic and named
dialogue; Kodlak's Journal). Varieties of Faith (readable in 4E) still lists the old Skyrim
pantheon as Alduin, Dibella, Orkey, Tsun, Mara, Stuhn, Kyne, Jhunal, Shor, Ysmir, Herma-Mora,
Mauloch -- a roster that shares Mara and Dibella with the Divines outright.

**"The Old Ways" is a live 4E 201 term with an in-game witness.** Froki Whetted-Blade, the
hermit hunter of Kyne's Sacred Trials, laments Divine worship, honors Kyne by her old name,
and dismisses Kynareth as a "pale shadow" of her (IN-GAME: Froki Whetted-Blade's dialogue,
Skyrim 4E; quest Kyne's Sacred Trials). He is the model of what an Old Ways Nord IS at 4E 201:
not a Dragon Cult revivalist, but a Nord who keeps the elder names, the hunt, and Sovngarde
against a temple establishment he finds thin. Old Ways identity texts circulate in 4E print:
Sovngarde, a Reexamination; A Dream of Sovngarde; the seditious pamphlet Nords Arise! (all
Skyrim 4E books).

**What "the Old Ways" is NOT at 4E 201.** The Atmoran totem religion and the Dragon Cult are
HISTORY to a 4E Nord -- described in the past tense by The Dragon War (Skyrim 4E book) and
met as murals and draugr, not as practice. The totem-to-god mapping (hawk=Kyne, fox=Shor,
whale=Stuhn, bear=Tsun, snake=Orkey, owl=Jhunal, moth=Dibella) rests on 4E fragments (Felldir
and Alduin's Bane dialogue name Kyne "Sister Hawk") plus 2E ESO codex text and The Song of
Gods (2E); use it as deep-history color, not as what a Whiterun Nord practices. Alduin's
return makes the old FEAR live again in 4E, but fear is not worship (section 6). The
Greybeards sit between the frames: they bestow "Ysmir, Dragon of the North" on the Last
Dragonborn "in the name of Kyne, in the name of Shor, and in the name of Atmora of Old"
(IN-GAME: Arngeir, Skyrim 4E) -- the strongest 4E proof that the old pantheon still acts in
the world under its own names.

## 2. Pantheon

Every god the mod's docs name for Nord, with domain, 4E 201 worship practice, and sources.

**Kyne (Kiss at the End, Mother of Men)** -- Nordic goddess of the Storm, widow of Shor,
favored god of warriors; her daughters taught the first Nords the Thu'um, the "Storm Voice"
(Varieties of Faith, 3E/4E). Nords hold that the sky exhaled onto the land at the Throat of
the World to form them (Varieties of Faith; Children of the Sky, 3E book). Practice at 4E 201:
Kyne's Sacred Trials -- hunting guardian beasts as devotion (IN-GAME: Froki, Skyrim 4E);
Kynesgrove's sacred trees; the Greybeards claim to act in her name (IN-GAME: Proclamation of
the Greybeards, Skyrim). Sacred animal: the hawk (Felldir the Old's dialogue, Skyrim 4E).
Kyne vs Kynareth: Nine Divines followers "consider them one and the same"; ardent Old Ways
Nords call Kynareth a pale shadow (Froki, Skyrim 4E). The lore therefore supports BOTH an
aspect reading and a distinct reading, and puts the split exactly along the mod's two lanes.
NOTE: the build keeps two distinct deity records (PDV_Deity_Kyne vs Imperial-owned
PDV_Deity_Kynareth, spec "Distinct from Kynareth"), i.e. distinct ledgers -- which matches the
Old Ways insider view, not a one-deity-two-skins model. See final-message flag.

**Shor (God of the Underworld)** -- the Nord Lorkhan; chief of the gods before his doom; sided
with Men, killed by Elven gods, now rules Sovngarde, which he built with his clever magic
(Varieties of Faith; The Road to Sovngarde, ESO 2E; Sovngarde, a Reexamination, Skyrim 4E).
The load-bearing practice fact: "Considered a 'dead god,' Shor has no priesthood and is not
actively worshiped, but he is frequently sworn by" (Varieties of Faith). At 4E 201 Sovngarde
is nonetheless demonstrably REAL -- the player walks it, sees Shor's empty throne and the
Hall of Valor (IN-GAME: Skyrim quest Sovngarde). Shor-devotion at 4E 201 is thus lived as
Sovngarde-aspiration: valor, honorable death, the mead-hall (Kodlak's Journal; A Dream of
Sovngarde; guard and Stormcloak dialogue). Totem: the fox (2E FLAVOR + Skyrim murals).

**Tsun** -- Nord god of trials against adversity; died defending Shor; VoF's classic edition
files him under "Dead God" -- "extinct" (Varieties of Faith; the ESO 2E excerpt uses the same
heading). But at 4E 201 Tsun is the single most CONCRETE Nord god: he stands at the Whalebone
Bridge as shield-thane and master of trials, tests arrivals in combat, and speaks (IN-GAME:
Tsun's dialogue, Skyrim 4E). No mortal temple or rite exists; his worship surface is the
trial itself. Totem: the bear (Divines and the Nords, ESO 2E; Skyrim murals).

**Stuhn (God of Ransom)** -- Nord precursor to Stendarr, brother of Tsun, shield-thane of
Shor; fought the Aldmeri pantheon and "showed Men how to take (and the benefits of taking)
prisoners of war" (Varieties of Faith). ESO adds "God of Ransom, Fraternity, and Justice"
(Shrine to Stuhn, ESO 2E) and a whale totem with a fishermen's "rite of the Whale" (ESO 2E
dialogue -- FLAVOR/2E only). ERA FLAG: Stuhn has essentially NO 4E 201 practice surface --
no Skyrim shrine, priest, or dialogue names him. His 4E presence is the Whalebone Bridge
itself and the VoF text. Thinnest of the shipped six, though his mercy/ransom domain is
mechanically distinctive.

**Talos / Ysmir (the Ninth)** -- hero-god of Mankind; in life Tiber Septim; ascended as the
Ninth Divine (Shezarr and the Divines, 3E; The Talos Mistake, 4E, hostile witness). Classic
VoF lists the Nord slot as YSMIR, "the Nordic aspect of Talos", the Dragon of the North --
a title also borne by Wulfharth and bestowed on the Last Dragonborn in 4E 201 (Varieties of
Faith; Arngeir's dialogue, Skyrim 4E). Practice at 4E 201 is defined by the ban: hidden and
hilltop shrines, contraband amulets, Heimskr preaching under Balgruuf's tolerance, open
worship only in Stormcloak Windhelm (IN-GAME: Skyrim events; Heimskr's dialogue; quest Return
to Grace context). The mod's "Talos/Ysmir" double-naming and its costly-defiance-only favor
rule are both well grounded.

**Arkay / Orkey** -- two names, one death-duty surface, and the mod's locked alias. Arkay:
Divine of the cycle of birth and death; at 4E 201 he owns Skyrim's actual death
infrastructure -- Halls of the Dead and their priests in Whiterun, Windhelm, Falkreath
(IN-GAME: Andurs, Helgird, Runil dialogue, Skyrim 4E). Orkey (Old Knocker): Nord god of
mortality, a "loan-god" combining aspects of Mauloch/Malacath and Arkay, taken up under
Aldmeri rule of Atmora; tricked the Nords into being "bound to the count of winters"; Shor
broke his six-year-lifespan curse and threw it onto the Orcs (Varieties of Faith). Orkey is
propitiated more than loved -- Divines and the Nords (ESO 2E) files him with the Testing Gods
one guards against, and ESO Nords keep house-god statues to appease such gods (2E FLAVOR) --
yet the same era shows an Orkey PRIESTHOOD tending Halls of the Dead (ESO 2E: Hall of the
Dead loading screen, Gromdred dialogue), and VoF:The Nords files him among the central Eight,
not the Testing Gods. By 4E 201 no Skyrim NPC invokes Orkey; the death-rites are Arkay's.
The alias ruling (Orkey as Old Ways display skin over Arkay internals) is therefore
era-honest: Orkey survives at 4E 201 as the elder NAME and the wary myth, while the practice
underneath is what the Empire calls Arkay. Copy assessment in section 5c. Totem: the snake.

**Mara** -- for Nords, "a handmaiden of Kyne and concubine of Shor", goddess of fertility and
agriculture (Varieties of Faith -- the Nord entry proper). Also the Empire's Mother-Goddess,
goddess of love and compassion, with a live 4E temple in Riften running the marriage rite
every Nord uses (IN-GAME: Maramal's dialogue and The Bond of Matrimony, Skyrim 4E). Mara is
genuinely in BOTH Nord frames: a named member of the old Skyrim pantheon AND a Divine. Her
Old Ways rank is subordinate (handmaiden, not head), which fits hearth-keeping texture better
than a marquee patron slot, but pantheon membership is unambiguous.

**Dibella (Goddess of Beauty)** -- named in the classic VoF SKYRIM pantheon list; "popular
god of the Eight Divines" with nearly a dozen cults -- women, artists, aesthetics, erotic
instruction (Varieties of Faith). Atmoran totem: the silver moth (Divines and the Nords, ESO
2E -- 2E FLAVOR for the totem claim). At 4E 201 her practice is the Markarth temple and the
Sybil tradition (IN-GAME: The Heart of Dibella, Skyrim 4E). Like Mara, she is honestly in
both frames; the guides naming her an Old Ways god is lore-supported.

**Akatosh, Stendarr, Zenithar, Julianos, Kynareth (the remaining Divines)** -- all live 4E
practice through shrines and institutions: Akatosh chief of the Divines (his Old Ways slot is
held by the FEARED Alduin -- section 6 -- so Akatosh-focus is a Divines-frame story only);
Stendarr's Vigilants (IN-GAME: Hall of the Vigilant, Skyrim 4E) with Stuhn as his named Nord
precursor -- "seen as the same" by some (Varieties of Faith; The Friend of All Mortals, ESO
2E), so the cross-lane mercy overlap is canonical; Zenithar via shrine blessing and trade
ethics, no Nord precursor named in VoF; Julianos via shrines and College-adjacent respect,
successor to the faded Jhunal (section 6); Kynareth -- goddess of the heavens, winds, and
unseen spirits of the air, patron of travelers (Varieties of Faith) -- via Whiterun's temple,
Danica Pure-Spring, and the Gildergreen rite (IN-GAME: The Blessings of Nature, Skyrim 4E).
For the Kyne/Kynareth identity question see the Kyne entry: Nine Divines Nords treat her as
Kyne under the temple name, per Froki, that equation's hostile 4E witness.

**Jhunal and Herma-Mora, the Nordic readings** -- Jhunal (Rune God): god of knowledge and
hermetic orders, precursor of Julianos, "never very popular among the mercurial and warlike
Nords, his worship is fading" (Varieties of Faith); owl totem; a 2E revival text exists
(Jhunal the Rune God, ESO 2E) but by 4E he is "largely absent in modern Skyrim mythology"
(UESP: Lore:Jhunal, resting on VoF). Herma-Mora (the Woodland Man): "Ancient Atmoran 'Demon
of Knowledge' who nearly seduced the Nords into becoming Aldmer"; most Ysgramor myths are
about escaping his wiles; Nords do not deny his Daedric nature (Varieties of Faith, Testing
Gods heading in the 2E excerpt). At 4E 201 the Skaal still fight "Herma-Mora, the demon of
knowledge" by that name (IN-GAME: Storn Crag-Strider, Dragonborn 4E). Both are the Nordic
readings of Divine-frame figures (Julianos, Hermaeus Mora); neither is a worship lane.

**Alduin (The World-Eater)** -- the Nord "variation" of Akatosh, but only superficially: the
firestorm that destroyed the last world to begin this one, both wellspring and apocalypse;
"the pantheon has no chief" -- Alduin is its grim source, not its king; once worshipped by
the outlawed Dragon Cult, "Alduin has no admitted worshipers" (all Varieties of Faith). At
4E 201 he is physically present and eating the honored dead's souls in Sovngarde (IN-GAME:
Skyrim main quest) -- the era's second live religious fact after the Talos ban.

## 3. The mod's lanes

Two broad baseline lanes chosen at startup, blended per-lane favors, focused patronage on
top (PDV_RaceDesign_Nord.md; PDV_NordRewardRecords.spec.json contractOverride).

**Broad Old Ways** -- History: the faith carried from Atmora, thinned by Imperialization but
alive in speech, Sovngarde-conviction, and hunters like Froki. Values and style: deeds over
kneeling -- the sky, the fight, the crypt, and the hidden shrine ARE the practice (Shor and
Tsun canonically need no temples). PLAYER FANTASY: you live the mythic, hold-born life and
the old gods turn out to still be watching; Sovngarde is a destination, not a metaphor. Play
should feel weathered, deed-led, and slightly outlawed.

**Broad Nine Divines** -- History: the Imperial cult as Skyrim institutionalized it --
temples in the holds, Halls of the Dead, marriage at Mara's altar -- now carrying a
Talos-shaped hole. Values and style: temple-named, priest-mediated, civically readable piety
that stays Nord in texture (holds, weather, family, death, honor). PLAYER FANTASY: you are
the respectable believer whose faith has a banned room in it -- temple piety on the surface,
and the question of what you carry underneath. Play should feel steadier, public, and
morally legible.

**Focused patronage (both lanes)** -- the Nord signature: you do not pick a god; a god
claims you at dawn after sustained aligned deeds. Lore support: Nordic religion is
deed-recognition throughout -- Kyne's trials are hunted not prayed, Sovngarde is earned by
valor, the Greybeards bestow recognition when deserved. The fantasy is being NOTICED.

## 4. God-to-lane associations, with evidence

Graded lore-strong vs thin-but-defensible (the Redguard convention). The headline finding:
the sources place Mara and Dibella in BOTH frames, place Talos across both, and make Shor
and Tsun officially "dead gods" whose 4E reality Skyrim itself upgrades -- so the lane map
is solid but two of the shipped six patrons out-run VoF's own worship-practice notes.

**Kyne as the Old Ways marquee: lore-strong.** Chief-deity readings exist (Divines and the
Nords, 2E; Goddess of Storm, Mother of Nords), the Thu'um is hers, the 4E witness (Froki)
worships her by name and disdains the temple skin. Nothing in the record resists it.

**Talos focusable in both lanes: lore-strong.** Old Ways: Ysmir holds a named seat in the
classic VoF Skyrim pantheon; the title is bestowed in 4E 201 in Kyne's and Shor's names.
Nine Divines: he IS the Ninth; the empty Solitude alcove makes his absence the loudest thing
in the temple. The two lanes give the same god two costumes -- ancestral defiance vs a
contradiction carried inside public faith -- which the race sheet already states as the
Talos pressure rule.

**Shor and Tsun as focusable Old Ways patrons: thin-but-defensible, defensible via 4E.**
AGAINST: VoF is explicit -- Shor has no priesthood and is not actively worshipped; Tsun is
filed under "Dead God"/"extinct". No 4E shrine, priest, or rite exists for either. FOR: at
4E 201 both are walkably real (Sovngarde quest), Tsun personally administers trials, and
Sovngarde-aspiration saturates live Nord speech (Companions, Stormcloaks, guards). A
"patron" here is honestly a RELATIONSHIP WITH THE HALL -- valor watched from Sovngarde --
not a cult. Copy should keep that shading (the shipped copy largely does: "Sovngarde is
closer than the map suggests").

**Stuhn as focusable: the thinnest shipped slot.** All practice evidence is 2E or 3E-text
(VoF entry; ESO shrine and whale-rite). Nothing in 4E Skyrim names him. Defensible as Tsun's
brother in the same hall-relationship frame, and his ransom/mercy domain is mechanically
distinct (spare, free, ransom) -- but a lore audit should call him what he is: a
reconstruction the mod is deliberately reviving. The Stendarr-identity line in VoF is the
mitigation: his domain is continuously worshipped at 4E under the name Stendarr.

**Orkey/Arkay in Old Ways: lore-strong AS AN ALIAS, exactly as locked.** The 4E death-duty
practice is Arkay's; Orkey is the surviving elder name with a warier theology (loan-god,
Testing-God filings, count-of-winters myth). One record, two skins matches the sources
better than a split deity would. See 5c for the copy question.

**Mara and Dibella in Old Ways: lore-supported membership, subordinate rank.** Both sit in
the classic VoF Skyrim pantheon list; Mara's Nord entry is explicitly relational to Kyne and
Shor (handmaiden/concubine). Neither is Old Ways-EXCLUSIVE -- their 4E temples are Divine
institutions. Verdict: naming them Old Ways gods (guides :5/:34/:221; Race_Nord.md:9) is not
a lore error; the drift is that the mechanics behind the naming do not exist (section 5a).

**The seven Divines for a Nine Divines Nord: lore-strong as a group.** A 4E Nord under the
Divines who is claimed by Mara, Kynareth, or Stendarr is not an Imperial transplant -- the
priests of these very temples are mostly Nords, and the race sheet's Nine Divines rule
(same Nord deed-surface, temple names) matches how Skyrim presents them. Akatosh, Zenithar,
Julianos are the thinnest INDIVIDUALLY for Nord texture (no Nord-specific reading beyond
the faded Jhunal for Julianos), but as Divines-frame patrons they are unremarkable and safe.

## 5. Options analysis: the contested slots

### 5a. The focusable roster (the wave's central question)

Ground truth (2026-08-27 drift survey -- trust over any doc): code ships SIX focused reward
families (Arkay, Kyne, Shor, Stuhn, Talos, Tsun) plus the two broad lanes (NineDivines_T1/T2,
OldWays_T1/T2); FOURTEEN offer messages are declared and returned by
PDV_OriginRuntime_Nord.psc (GetNordFormalCommitmentOfferMessage, :666ff), covering all
thirteen roster gods plus the Orkey skin of Arkay; and IsNordOfferEligibleDeity (:709ff)
already implements a LANE-SPLIT eligibility rule -- Old Ways: Kyne/Shor/Tsun/Stuhn/Mara/
Arkay-as-Orkey/Dibella, Nine Divines: the eight Divines, Talos in both. (The offer GUARD
asserting only Kyne is a known gate defect being filed separately; it is not roster truth.)
The spec superseded its old roster rule with "Nord can FOCUS ANY GOD IN ITS CHOSEN PANTHEON"
(PDV_NordRewardRecords.spec.json:24, DECISION 2026-06-07). So the offer surface and the
spec agree with each other, and both disagree with the shipped reward families: seven gods
(Mara, Dibella, Akatosh, Stendarr, Zenithar, Julianos, Kynareth) can be OFFERED and accepted
with no focused blessing family behind the acceptance.

**Lore assessment of who a 4E 201 Nord would credibly take as patron:** Old Ways -- Kyne,
Talos/Ysmir, and the Sovngarde hall-relationship (Shor, Tsun, Stuhn) are the credible
marquee patrons; Orkey-as-Arkay is credible as crypt-duty; Mara and Dibella are credible but
subordinate (hearth and hall rather than marquee). Nine Divines -- all eight Divines plus
Talos are credible by 4E temple practice, with Mara/Kynareth/Stendarr/Arkay the most Nord-
textured and Akatosh/Zenithar/Julianos/Dibella carried by generic Divine practice.

**(i) Roster = the six with families; trim offers to match.**
- Lore: defensible for Old Ways only by dropping Mara/Orkey-name/Dibella offers (retaining
  the Orkey-skinned Arkay family). For Nine Divines it is NOT defensible: the lane would
  offer only Talos and Arkay, gutting the frame whose whole identity is the eight temples.
  The player guide's Divines lane promise ("you can be claimed by Akatosh, Mara, ...") would
  be false.
- Mechanics: cheapest build -- delete/park eight offer messages, align the eligibility gate,
  delete the seven phantom manifest families. No new records.
- Risk: reopens the spec's superseding 2026-06-07 decision in the restrictive direction and
  makes the two lanes asymmetric in kind (Old Ways gets patrons, Divines gets a cap).

**(ii) Roster = all fourteen offers; build the seven missing families.**
- Lore: fully supported (section 4) -- this is the only option under which the guides' god
  lists become true as written.
- Mechanics -- name the cost honestly: seven reward families (T1/T2/T3 SPEL + MGEFs each,
  ~21 SPELs plus effects), Recover-flag invariants, magnitude ceilings, offer/accept wiring
  already present, manifest copy already DRAFTED (the :167-189 rows are phantom but usable
  as specs), plus neglect text, Prisma keys, journey-board rows. MITIGATION the spec itself
  documents: the Mara entry (spec:97) already prescribes REUSING Imperial families
  (PDV_Bless_Imperial_Mara_T1/T2/T3 via SyncNordRewardFamily baseline -1) -- if that
  mechanism is wired, the seven Divines-lane families are wiring work, not record-authoring
  work. Verify whether SyncNordRewardFamily reuse is live before costing this option as a
  full build. The Orkey lesson applies in reverse: for Divines-lane gods the Imperial names
  ARE the correct display, so reuse has no Active-Effects naming problem; only Old Ways
  Mara/Dibella would show temple-named effects inside the mythic frame (tolerable -- the
  names are identical across frames, unlike Orkey/Arkay).
- Risk: seven more patrons to balance and neglect-texture; Akatosh/Zenithar/Julianos have
  the least Nord-specific material to write from.

**(iii) Lane-split roster (Old Ways gods focusable in Old Ways, Divines in Nine Divines).**
- This is not a third architecture -- it is what the spec rule ("any god in its CHOSEN
  pantheon") and the shipped eligibility gate ALREADY say. The real content of (iii) is
  (ii)'s family work scoped per lane, with the cross-lane memberships resolved: Mara and
  Dibella focusable in both lanes (as the gate ships), Talos in both (as shipped), Kyne vs
  Kynareth kept as the lane-respective names.
- Lore: the strongest fit of the three -- it mirrors Froki's actual theology (same forces,
  two languages, and your chosen frame determines which names can claim you).
- Mechanics: identical family cost to (ii) minus nothing; plus a small copy task -- the
  guides already describe exactly this model.

**RECOMMENDATION (not a decision): (iii), which subsumes (ii).** The superseding spec rule,
the shipped offer gate, and the player guides already describe the lane-split
any-god-in-your-pantheon model; the lore supports it; and the missing piece is seven reward
families whose specs (manifest :167-189) and whose reuse mechanism (spec:97) both already
exist on paper. Option (i) should only win if the owner explicitly wants to shrink the Nord
promise, and it cannot honestly keep the Nine Divines lane. If (iii) is ruled, the phantom
manifest rows stop being drift and become the build's copy source; if (i) is ruled, they are
deleted with tombstones pointing here.

### 5b. Focused presentation for the seven Divines, or broad-only texture?

Lore does not support broad-only as the "honest Nord frame": 4E Skyrim shows Nords in
individual Divine devotion constantly (Danica's Kynareth crisis, Maramal's Mara evangelism,
the Vigilants' Stendarr militancy). What IS true is that a Divines-frame Nord patron should
stay Nord in deed-surface (the race sheet's Nine Divines rule) rather than inheriting
Imperial civic texture -- under (iii)/(ii) each Divine needs only a light Nord skin over the
shared family. Under (i) this question becomes acute: the guide's Divines lane must be
rewritten around a broad-only promise. Recommendation: follow 5a; do not decide 5b
independently.

### 5c. Orkey copy treatment

The lock (spec:25) is correct and era-honest (section 2, Arkay/Orkey). The remaining
question is TONE. Current copy (player guide :23) reads "the old death-god, met through
proper burial, crypt duty, and respect for the dead" -- a warm keeper-of-the-dead framing
that is really Arkay's. The sources give Orkey a colder edge: a Testing God some Nords
appease rather than love (Divines and the Nords, 2E), the god who "bound them to the count
of winters" (Varieties of Faith), snake-totemed, "None may fool wary Orkey" (The Song of
Gods, 2E). RECOMMENDED copy posture (not decided): keep the death-duty deed surface
identical to Arkay's, but let Old Ways Orkey copy carry wariness -- rites done so the count
of winters stays honest and the dead stay down, duty owed to a god you respect rather than
embrace. One caveat the other way: the 2E Orkey PRIESTHOOD tending Halls of the Dead shows
the propitiation reading was never the whole story, so the copy should be wary, not
demonized -- VoF:The Nords files him with the central gods, not the Testing Gods.

## 6. Considered and rejected (non-focusable by design)

Recorded so future greps hit an explanation rather than a gap.

- **Jhunal** -- scholar-god already faded by the mod's era: "never very popular... his
  worship is fading" (Varieties of Faith); absorbed into Julianos. The honest 4E path for a
  scholarly Nord is Julianos in the Divines lane; Jhunal is Old Ways texture at most
  (owl-totem color), never a patron.
- **Alduin** -- "no admitted worshipers" (Varieties of Faith); at 4E 201 he is the live
