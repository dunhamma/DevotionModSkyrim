# PDV Redguard Lore Baseline

**Status: LIVING**
**Date:** 2026-08-25
**Purpose:** Sourced Redguard/Yokudan lore baseline for the Phase B Forebear-slot decision and
future copy work. Lore claims cite the in-game text they rest on. Claims that rest only on
out-of-game material (developer Q&A, Loremaster's Archive, printed guides) are flagged as such.
This document records evidence and options; it decides nothing. Owner rulings land in the final
section.

**Source-tier legend:**
- IN-GAME: readable book or spoken dialogue inside a shipped TES game (Daggerfall, Redguard,
  Morrowind/Oblivion/Skyrim, ESO).
- FLAVOR: in-game item/antiquity/codex description text (canon but thin).
- OOG: official out-of-game material (Pocket Guide print editions, Loremaster's Archive,
  developer Q&A). Flagged wherever load-bearing.

## 1. Pantheon

The backbone source is Varieties of Faith in the Empire (Brother Mikhael Karkuxor; UESP:
Lore:Varieties of Faith...), which carries a one-entry writeup for every god below. ESO ships
two sect-split versions of the same text -- Varieties of Faith, Crown Redguards and Varieties
of Faith, The Forebears -- which are the single most load-bearing texts for section 3. The
creation myth is The Monomyth, "Satakal the Worldskin" section (UESP: Lore:The Monomyth).

**Ruptga (Tall Papa)** -- Chief deity of the Yokudan pantheon. First god to survive the Hunger
of Satakal; taught the others the Walkabout; set the stars in the sky so lesser spirits could
find their way; created Sep as a helper (Varieties of Faith in the Empire; The Monomyth,
"Satakal the Worldskin"). Worship practice: purple prayer beads, star motifs -- "Ruptga without
stars is like Akatosh without a Dragon" (FLAVOR: ESO Tall Papa's Lamp / Starry Tapestry
descriptions; UESP: Lore:Ruptga). He is a cosmological father figure, not a deeds-and-favor
patron in any sourced practice.

**Satakal (The Worldskin)** -- Yokudan god of everything; fusion of Anu and Padomay; eats
itself and sheds its skin to begin each new world; the pantheon is the spirits who learned to
survive the cycle (Varieties of Faith in the Empire; The Monomyth). Popular god of the Alik'r
nomads (Varieties of Faith in the Empire). Practice: serpent iconography on Alik'r maps,
priesthoods, and a fringe "Satakals" sect that rolls sidewinder-fashion across the desert (OOG:
Pocket Guide to the Empire, 1st Ed., Hammerfell; FLAVOR: ESO Satakal Skinrazer codex). Satakal
is cosmology first: the frame the other gods live inside, not a god you petition for outcomes.

**Tu'whacca (Tricky God)** -- Yokudan god of souls; caretaker of the Far Shores; before
creation he was "the god of Nobody Really Cares" until the Walkabout gave him a purpose; guides
Redguards into the afterlife and outwits the enemies of a soul in passage (Varieties of Faith,
The Forebears; Tu'whacca's Prayer; When We Pass). His cult is associated with Arkay in
cosmopolitan Forebear regions "where the names Tu'whacca and Arkay are basically
interchangeable" (Varieties of Faith, The Forebears); Crowns detest that Imperialization
(A Betrayal of Our Heritage). Practice: consecration of the dead, mummification rites
(The Eight Steps of Mummification), ibis iconography and priest masks, the temple at
Tu'whacca's Throne in the Alik'r (ESO: Throne Keeper Farvad dialogue). Three Ansei sacrificed
their souls to him to create the Ansei Wards, which keep the royal dead from being raised
(IN-GAME: ESO, Throne Keeper Farvad dialogue; quest Restoring the Ansei Wards).

**Leki (Saint of the Spirit Sword)** -- Goddess of aberrant swordsmanship, daughter of Tall
Papa. Broke the deadlock of the Na-Totambu swordmasters by introducing the Ephemeral Feint,
which let a victor emerge and the war with the Lefthanded Elves begin (Varieties of Faith in
the Empire). Said to reside in the Far Shores; the settlement Leki's Blade was founded where
she personally dueled Rada al-Saran (IN-GAME: ESO Rahama and Rada al-Saran dialogue). The
sword-singer tradition -- Ansei, the Shehai, the Halls of the Virtues of War, Frandar
Hunding's Book of Circles -- is her cultural lane (Divad the Singer; From The Memory Stone of
Makela Leki; Redguards, History and Heroes; all Daggerfall in-game books).

**HoonDing (The Make Way God)** -- Yokudan spirit of "perseverance over infidels"; materializes
whenever the Redguards need to "make way" for their people. The classic text counts three
manifestations -- twice in the First Era during the Ra Gada invasion, once during the Tiber
War, when the HoonDing "was said to have been either a sword or a crown, or both" (Varieties
of Faith in the Empire; the sword/crown reads as Prince A'tor's Soul Sword and/or Cyrus, per
the game Redguard). The ESO sect versions count only the two Ra Gada-era manifestations
(Varieties of Faith, Crown Redguards; Varieties of Faith, The Forebears) -- the count is
edition-dependent; the pattern (rare, historic, people-scale crisis) is not. Diagna is a
HoonDing avatar that achieved permanence (Varieties of Faith in the Empire); Frandar Hunding
is named an aspect of the HoonDing only in FLAVOR text (ESO contraband: Hoonding's Golden
Quill, Hoonding's Prayer Stone). He is an event, not a shrine cult: no sourced daily worship
practice exists.

**Tava (Bird God)** -- Yokudan spirit of the air. Most famous for leading the Yokudans to the
isle of Herne after the destruction of their homeland; since assimilated into the mythology of
Kynareth; "still very popular in Hammerfell among sailors, and her shrines can be found in
most port cities" (Varieties of Faith in the Empire, identical in both ESO sect versions).
Practice: shrines in port cities; priests bless astrolabes for royal naval officers (FLAVOR:
ESO Blessed Astrolabe); redtail hawk and goshawk sacred (The Worthy Ar-Azal, His Deeds); the
second Ra Gada "Tavan wave" bore bird imagery on arms and armor (Crafting Motif 28: Ra Gada
Style). Lainlyn was once named "Tava's Blessing" (OOG: ESO Ask Us Anything). Domain over sun
and storm, all air but not the stars (OOG: The Unveiled Azadiyeh Answers Your Questions,
Loremaster's Archive).

**Onsi (Boneshaver)** -- Notable warrior god of the Yokudan Ra Gada; "taught Mankind how to
pull their knives into swords" (Varieties of Faith in the Empire; Sword-Wisdom of Saikhalar).
The Book of Circles prescribes the "Oblation of Onsi" on completing a forged sword (per UESP:
Lore:Onsi). Probably the same figure PGE1 calls the god "Ansei" (OOG inference: Pocket Guide
1st Ed., Hammerfell). His lane -- swords, forging, martial teaching -- sits almost entirely
inside Leki's.

**Zeht (God of Farms)** -- Yokudan god of agriculture. "Renounced his father after the world
was created, which is why Tall Papa makes it so hard to grow food" (Varieties of Faith in the
Empire; the Forebear version comically swaps in Akatosh as the withholding father). The
disowning is mutual estrangement inside the family, not expulsion from the pantheon: he keeps
a seat in the Crown Eight (Varieties of Faith, Crown Redguards). Analogous to Zenithar and
sometimes worshipped in that name by Forebears (Varieties of Faith, The Forebears). Practice:
blessed farming tools, sacred white camels (FLAVOR: ESO items).

**Morwha (Teat God)** -- Yokudan fertility goddess, favorite of Tall Papa's wives, always
portrayed four-armed "so that she can grab more husbands" (Varieties of Faith in the Empire).
Practice: beehive-shaped shrines (Crafting Motif 6: Redguard Style), marriage officiation by
her priestesses (ESO dialogue). Note for Ash'abah color: the ESO Ash'abah camp is named
Morwha's Bounty (ESO location).

**Diagna (Orichalc God of the Sideways Blade)** -- "An avatar of the HoonDing ... that
achieved permanence." Brought orichalc weapons to defeat the Lefthanded Elves; in Tamriel led
the Order of Diagna against old Orsinium; "now little more than a local power spirit of the
Dragontail Mountains" (all Varieties of Faith in the Empire). He is the precedent that
HoonDing manifestations can individualize -- and also the precedent that they then fade.

**Sep (The Snake)** -- Yokudan version of Lorkhan. Created by Tall Papa as a helper; maddened
by Satakal's hunger; tricked spirits into building the mortal world from dead skins, stranding
them too far to jump to the Far Shores; punished -- Tall Papa "squashed the Snake with a big
stick" -- and persists only as a hungry void that "tries to upset mortal entry into the Far
Shores" (The Monomyth, "Satakal the Worldskin"; Varieties of Faith in the Empire). Sep is the
pantheon's cautionary error: the reason mortality exists, an obstacle to the afterlife, and at
no sourced point a worship target.

**Malooc (Horde King)** -- "An enemy god of the Ra Gada." Led the goblins against the
Redguards in the First Era; fled east when the army of the HoonDing overtook his hordes
(Varieties of Faith in the Empire). An adversary in Redguard myth, not a Redguard god; UESP
identifies him with Malacath (UESP: Lore:Malooc redirect -- wiki synthesis, not an in-game
equation).

## 2. The three sect cultures

Lore honesty note: Crowns and Forebears are genuine society-wide factions. The Ash'abah are a
specific outcast tribe of the Alik'r, not a sect anyone historically "joins" at a temple. The
mod generalizes the Ash'abah into a chooseable path; that is a design extension, defensible
because the tribe's function (bearing the impurity of undead-slaying for everyone else) is
exactly the fantasy the mod needs a third lane for.

**Crown** -- History: the Na-Totambu, Yokuda's old ruling class, who arrived after the Ra Gada
had cleared the way and re-established their hereditary rule at Hegathe (Pocket Guide 1st Ed.,
Hammerfell -- OOG print; The Na-Totambu of Yokuda, ESO). Values: continuity of Yokudan law,
rank, and religion; they "still revere the ancient Yokudan gods" (Varieties of Faith, Crown
Redguards) and regard Forebear syncretism as "a betrayal of our heritage" (A Betrayal of Our
Heritage). Worship style: the full old pantheon by its own names, temple and rite kept as they
were on Yokuda. PLAYER FANTASY: you are the orthodoxy carried intact into exile -- the player
who keeps the old names in a province that has never heard them, and is watched approvingly by
ancestors for it. Crown play should feel like bearing, form, and inheritance: doing things
correctly matters more than doing them conveniently.

**Forebear** -- History: descendants of the Ra Gada, the Warrior Wave that landed first,
cleared the coasts, and only later saw their old rulers arrive to reclaim the top (Pocket
Guide 1st Ed., Hammerfell -- OOG print; ESO Ramati at-Gar dialogue names Yokeda Razul the
first Forebear). Values: adaptation and engagement -- the most cosmopolitan Redguards, traders
and dealers with foreigners (The Improved Emperor's Guide to Tamriel: Hammerfell -- OOG
print), who "worship substantially the same pantheon as the Imperials and Bretons" while
keeping Tava, Tu'whacca, Zeht, and Morwha under Yokudan names inside it (Varieties of Faith,
The Forebears). They are also the sect said to quietly support the Ash'abah and call on them
for aid (Sacrilege and Mayhem in the Alik'r). PLAYER FANTASY: you make a life among outsiders
and stay Redguard doing it -- the pragmatist whose gods answer to more than one name, who
takes the contract, walks the road, and never mistakes flexibility for surrender. Forebear
play should feel like earned passage through a foreign world.

**Ash'abah** -- History: born at Ash'abah Pass ca. 1E 853, when a son of King Xakhwan slew his
father's reanimated corpse and accepted exile for it -- "the first sacrifice" (IN-GAME: ESO
quest The Initiation; Prince Haqmir and Vizier Yeqdah dialogue). "Unclean people" who slay
what might be honored ancestors returned (The Improved Emperor's Guide to Tamriel: Hammerfell
-- OOG print; translation per ESO Thieves Guild Q&A -- OOG). Values: the body is sacred; the
risen dead must be put down, ritually, with pleas of forgiveness (Correct Ways of Slaying
Ra-Netu, ESO). Worship style: purification rituals for mausoleums, an annual ceremony at
Tu'whacca's Throne for the royal dead -- "regarded as blasphemous, but tolerated due to the
necessity of its performance" -- and the whole of society calling on them secretly when the
dead rise, as Fahara'jad did against Suturah and again against the Withered Hand (Sacrilege
and Mayhem in the Alik'r; ESO Talia at-Marimah dialogue; quest Restoring the Ansei Wards).
PLAYER FANTASY: you carry what others will not touch -- the player who does the necessary,
thankless, contaminating work, shunned by the people it protects and honored only by the god
of the dead himself. Ash'abah play should feel narrow, costly, and quietly acknowledged.

## 3. God-to-sect associations, with evidence

The single most important finding of this baseline: the sources actively resist a 1:1
sect-to-god mapping. Both ESO sect versions of Varieties of Faith carry an identical
"Additional Deities with Significant Redguard Cults" list -- Leki, HoonDing, Malooc, Sep --
meaning the two most gameplay-relevant gods (Leki, HoonDing) are canonically pan-sect cults,
and Tava/Tu'whacca appear in BOTH sects' Eights.

**Tava as the Forebear-associated goddess: lore-thin-but-defensible.**
- FOR: Tava holds a named seat in the Forebear Eight and is "often worshiped by the Forebears
  in that name [Kynareth]" (Varieties of Faith, The Forebears) -- one of only four Yokudan
  gods the Forebears kept by name. Her constituency is sailors and her shrines stand "in most
  port cities" (Varieties of Faith in the Empire), and the port cities -- Sentinel, Rihad,
  Taneth -- are the Forebear strongholds (OOG: Pocket Guide 3rd Ed., Hammerfell). Coastal,
  cosmopolitan, travel-coded: the fit with the mod's Forebear texture is real.
- AGAINST: she holds the same named seat in the Crown Eight (Varieties of Faith, Crown
  Redguards), and her flagship shrine at Hegathe -- a Crown capital -- is "a place of
  veneration for both the Crowns and the Forebears," its Fresco of the Goshawk painted to
  symbolize the UNION of the two factions (The Worthy Ar-Azal, His Deeds). No source calls
  Tava a Forebear goddess. The association is an inference from geography and constituency,
  not a stated allegiance.
- Verdict: defensible as texture ("Tava leads" the Forebear road lane, as the race sheet
  already has it); NOT strong enough that a sourced-lore argument compels a Tava-Forebear
  focused deity.

**HoonDing as pan-Redguard and Ra Gada/conquest-coded: lore-strong.**
- He is in the additional-cults list of BOTH sect books (Varieties of Faith, Crown Redguards;
  Varieties of Faith, The Forebears). His manifestations are people-scale: twice for the
  Ra Gada invasion -- which makes him conquest-coded and ancestral to the FOREBEAR founding
  story specifically -- and once (classic text only) in the Tiber War, where the incarnation
  was the Soul Sword / Cyrus, both CROWN-side figures (Varieties of Faith in the Empire; the
  game Redguard). His avatar Diagna served all Redguards against Orsinium (Varieties of Faith
  in the Empire). The god of make-way appears wherever the PEOPLE need a way made, on either
  sect's side of history.
- Verdict: the sources support HoonDing-as-pan-Redguard better than HoonDing-as-Forebear. The
  mod's current "best fit for Forebear" framing is the weakest of its three sect-fit claims.

**Leki and the Crown lane (sword-singer inheritance): lore-thin-but-defensible.**
- FOR: sword-singing is the ancient tradition -- Ansei, Shehai, the Book of Circles, the Halls
  of the Virtues of War (Divad the Singer; From The Memory Stone of Makela Leki; Redguards,
  History and Heroes) -- and the Crowns are the sect defined by keeping ancient tradition
  (The Na-Totambu of Yokuda; Varieties of Faith, Crown Redguards). Leki's founding myth is
  even set among the Na-Totambu swordmasters (Varieties of Faith in the Empire).
- AGAINST: Leki is in both sect books' cult lists and "one of the most popular gods in
  Hammerfell" outright (OOG: Pocket Guide 1st Ed., Hammerfell). Sword-singing predates the
  sect split entirely; Makela Leki herself died defending a Sentinel-Daggerfall alliance.
- Verdict: Crown-flavored by way of tradition-keeping, but the blade belongs to everyone. The
  mod's "best fit for Crown and Forebear" copy is already honest here.

**Tu'whacca and the Ash'abah (death-duty): lore-strong.**
- The Ash'abah's signature rite is held at Tu'whacca's Throne (The Improved Emperor's Guide --
  OOG print, corroborated IN-GAME by the ESO Alik'r quest chain at the Throne); the Ansei
  Wards that guard the royal dead are a covenant with Tu'whacca (ESO Throne Keeper Farvad
  dialogue); their killing rituals exist to free the body from necromancy, which is precisely
  Tu'whacca's soul-passage domain (Correct Ways of Slaying Ra-Netu).
- Caveat: Tu'whacca is simultaneously the most pan-Redguard god of all -- in both sect Eights,
  in every deathbed idiom (Sacrilege and Mayhem in the Alik'r). The death-duty tie is strong;
  Ash'abah exclusivity would not be. The mod's "best fit for Crown and Ash'abah" framing is
  compatible with the sources.

## 4. Options analysis: the Forebear slot

Context: R5 in PDV_RedguardRewardRecords.spec.json locks focusable records to
Tu'whacca/HoonDing/Leki; the 2026-07-08 divergence ledger locks Satakal/Ruptga/Tava/Onsi/Zeht
as broad-only flavor "unless owner-reopened later." Options (b) and (c) are exactly such a
reopening; that is what Phase B is for.

**(a) Keep HoonDing as-is; reframe him pan-Redguard. Documentation sweep only.**
- Lore: best supported option. Both sect books list him as a pan-sect cult; his manifestations
  serve Crowns and Forebears alike (section 3). Dropping "best fit for Forebear" in favor of
  "the god who appears for the whole people, whichever road you walk" is MORE lore-accurate
  than the current copy, not a compromise.
- Mechanics: keeps the shipped make-way signal family, which fires on dragon and named-boss
  kills -- events the engine detects cleanly (Story Manager kill events, verified hooks per
  the race sheet's build-facing hook table). No record, script, or signal work.
- Cost: player guide, race sheet, journey board, and Prisma display copy sweep. No ESP change.
- Risk: the Forebear player keeps a patron whose sect-coded identity is looser; Forebear
  differentiation then rests on the broad lane (Tava/Zeht texture) rather than the focus god.

**(b) Recast the Forebear slot under Tava. Full deity build.**
- Lore: defensible (Forebear Eight seat, sailors, port cities) but the same texts put her in
  the Crown Eight and make her Hegathe shrine a both-sect unity symbol -- a Tava-for-Forebears
  build would out-run its sources on exclusivity, though not on relevance.
- Mechanics -- the decisive problem: Tava's natural hooks are travel-based, and travel is the
  weakest signal class in this codebase by the design doc's own words: the Forebear road lane
  "must reject fast travel and short local movement" (PDV_RaceDesign_Redguard.md, pilot table)
  and the implementation notes flag the same fast-travel concern as Khajiit road-travel.
  Weather/storm hooks add Survival-Mode coupling. Meanwhile HoonDing's boss/dragon-kill signal
  is among the cleanest the engine offers. Recasting trades the strongest detection surface in
  the Redguard slate for the weakest.
- Cost: full deity build -- deity record + stance wiring, 3-tier reward ladder (T1/T2/T3 SPEL
  + MGEFs within ledger ceilings), offer records, a new signal family with anti-farm caps,
  manager properties, FLST growth, quest-matrix rows, Prisma display keys, journey-board and
  guide copy; plus retiring or re-homing the shipped HoonDing records and his quest-matrix
  rows (HoonDing would presumably remain focusable for other sects, so this is a re-keying,
  not a deletion). Requires reopening R5 and the divergence-ledger ruling.

**(c) Build Tava as a FOURTH focusable deity alongside the three. Roster growth.**
- Lore: same evidence as (b), minus the exclusivity problem -- a pan-sect Tava that anyone can
  focus is actually the most source-faithful way to make her focusable.
- Mechanics: identical build cost to (b) (every item except the HoonDing re-keying), PLUS
  roster-balance questions: 4 gods over 3 sects breaks the sect-symmetric presentation, the
  offer gate and dawn evaluation now arbitrate four candidates, broad-vs-focused suppression
  and the one-favor rule need re-verification against a fourth family, and every surface that
  enumerates "the three you can commit to" (player guide, journey boards, Prisma keys, R5)
  changes. And the weak-signal-class problem from (b) is not avoided -- a focusable god still
  needs a farmproof signal family to be earnable.

**RECOMMENDATION (not a decision): (a).** The sources say the sect-to-god mapping was never
1:1, so the cheapest option is also the most lore-honest one; and both Tava options spend a
full deity build to acquire the codebase's weakest signal class. If the owner wants Tava
elevated anyway, (c) is the more source-faithful shape than (b), at strictly higher cost than
(a). Reopening R5 and the 2026-07-08 ledger ruling is a Phase B owner call in either case.

## 5. Considered and rejected (non-focusable by design)

These gods are deliberately NOT focusable patrons in PDV. Recorded here so future greps hit an
explanation rather than a gap. Locked by the 2026-07-08 divergence ledger (broad-only/flavor
unless owner-reopened) and R5 of the Redguard reward spec.

- **Satakal** -- cosmology, not a patron. The Worldskin is the frame the whole pantheon exists
  inside (The Monomyth); sourced worship is fringe cult practice, not deeds-and-favor. Broad
  spine flavor only.
- **Ruptga** -- pan-Yokudan chief god above sect politics; a father figure who sets stars, not
  a god who answers individual conduct. Folded into HoonDing make-way flavor per the ledger.
- **Tava** -- pan-sect texture goddess (both Eights; both-sect shrine at Hegathe); her natural
  hooks are travel/weather, the weakest signal class here. Leads the Forebear broad road lane
  as texture; focus status is the section 4 question, owner-gated.
- **Onsi** -- forging and martial teaching sit almost wholly inside Leki's shipped lane
  (forge/train/duel signals); a focusable Onsi would duplicate her surface. Deferred per the
  ledger.
- **Zeht** -- agriculture god with nearly no detectable Skyrim surface for a Redguard
  adventurer; his Zenithar parallel is already carried by the Forebear interpretive overlap
  and the broad trade rows.
- Never candidates: **Sep** (cautionary error and afterlife obstacle, not a worship target),
  **Malooc** (enemy god of the Ra Gada), **Diagna** (a faded HoonDing avatar, "little more
  than a local power spirit"), **Morwha** (fertility/marriage domain with no PDV mechanical
  surface; available as broad flavor and Ash'abah camp color).

## Appendix: source-reliability notes

- Best in-game anchors used here: Varieties of Faith in the Empire and its two ESO sect
  variants; The Monomyth; Sacrilege and Mayhem in the Alik'r; A Betrayal of Our Heritage;
  The Worthy Ar-Azal, His Deeds; Correct Ways of Slaying Ra-Netu; Tu'whacca's Prayer; When We
  Pass; Crafting Motif 28: Ra Gada Style; the Daggerfall sword-singer corpus (Divad the
  Singer; From The Memory Stone of Makela Leki; Redguards, History and Heroes); ESO Alik'r
  quest dialogue (The Initiation; Restoring the Ansei Wards; Throne Keeper Farvad; Talia
  at-Marimah).
- OOG material load-bearing anywhere above: Pocket Guide to the Empire 1st/3rd Ed. (printed
  in-universe guides; sect strongholds, Na-Totambu/Ra Gada history), The Improved Emperor's
  Guide to Tamriel (printed; Ash'abah social standing), Loremaster's Archive Q&As (Tava's
  domain edges), Ask Us Anything (Tava's Blessing). Ebonarm-as-HoonDing and Cyrus lore beyond
  the games rest on developer forum posts (UOL) and are NOT used as evidence here.
- Correction to this document's own brief: "Notes For Redguard History" (Daggerfall) is Destri
  Melarg's letter about Derik Hallin and the five swords -- it contains neither the
  Na-Totambu/Ra Gada sect origins nor the HoonDing manifestation count. Those claims live in
  the Pocket Guides and in Varieties of Faith respectively, and are cited accordingly above.
