## Cross-generation adjudication

The College seed pass produced 40 candidates. Primary review kept 13 narrow
profile matches and added seven Syrabane core-study cells. After those decisions
were incorporated, the full six-channel pass produced **68 remaining candidates
and zero conflicts**. All 68 were reviewed and rejected.

Standing rejection rules controlled the result:

- Generic `disciplined_study` echoes for Akatosh and Azura were not used to turn
  every College lesson into broad coverage. Julianos, Magnus, Auri-El, Syrabane,
  and lesson-specific domains already express the direct theology.
- Generic protection and lawful-order matches were not added to Treva's Watch
  merely because the outcome helps a family and restores a fort.
- Reversing Telrav's ambush was not inflated across every combat, struggle, and
  hero-god profile. The direct Shor, Malacath, and Baan Dar readings are enough.
- Trial by combat was not spread across every justice, duel, and chaos profile.
  Leki and Stendarr carry the direct contest and cleared-name result.
- Saving Madanach is morally mixed because the same completion says he will
  rebuild his army. The direct mercy/release reading is retained, but the Kyne
  cross-generation echo is rejected.
- No candidate was kept merely to reduce a paired-equity or reachability count.
