# Khajiit Five-Deity Daily Signal Audit -- Decision Packet

This audit records the current signal floor only. It does not change signal
magnitudes, anti-farm rules, or add new faucets.

| Deity | Renewable positive route | Cadence and control | Daily availability verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Khenarthi | Completed outdoor rest -> road-home focus and `SIGNAL_ROAD_HOME` | Once-per-day value after the first sleep; directly player-controlled | Strong |
| Azurah | Valid `Observe the Moons` rite -> raw `+0.4` piety for the god in strength | The rite is player-controlled, but Azurah receives it only in either of her two strength slots | Uneven |
| Baan Dar | Outnumbered low-health victory -> road trick; near-fatal reversal is weekly | Combat composition and adversity dependent; ordinary daily play cannot guarantee it | Thin |
| Rajhin | Organic elegant theft -> `SIGNAL_ELEGANT_THEFT` | Renewable and daily-capped, but requires a qualifying theft and a thief playstyle | Adequate for its lane, narrow overall |
| Alkosh | Dragon-order/generic dragon route, named dragon milestones, and dawn Word-of-Power drip | Generic dragon kills renew, while named dragons and Words are finite; not a dependable daily practice | Thin |

## Decision

The five paths are mechanically reachable, but they do not have equal daily
agency. Khenarthi has a dependable daily practice. Rajhin has a dependable one
for a committed thief. Azurah is reliable only when her strength slot is active.
Baan Dar and Alkosh depend on encounter availability and are the clearest risk
of falling behind in ordinary sessions.

Do not add faucets in this tranche. Before the all-race balance pass, decide
whether the product goal is strict daily parity or intentionally different
religious rhythms. If strict parity is desired, investigate one lore-specific,
player-owned routine for Baan Dar and Alkosh and a non-phase-exclusive threshold
practice for Azurah. Any proposal must remain non-generic, daily-capped, and
separate from the `+4` lunar substrate budget.
