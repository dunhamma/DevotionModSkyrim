# PDV Breton Lore Baseline

**Status: LIVING**
**Date:** 2026-08-27
**Purpose:** Sourced Breton lore baseline for the Wave 1 rebaseline decisions (champion model,
Phynaster disposition, Daedric-legibility expression) and future copy work. Lore claims cite the
in-game text they rest on. Claims resting only on out-of-game material are flagged OOG. This
document records evidence and options; it decides nothing. Owner rulings land in the final section.

**Source-tier legend:**
- IN-GAME: readable book or spoken dialogue inside a shipped TES game (Arena, Daggerfall,
  Morrowind/Oblivion/Skyrim, ESO).
- FLAVOR: in-game item/antiquity/codex description text (canon but thin).
- OOG: official out-of-game material (Pocket Guide print editions, Improved Emperor's Guide,
  Loremaster's Archive, novels, developer Q&A). Flagged wherever load-bearing.

**Era discipline:** the mod is set in 4E 201. Daggerfall is 3E 405; Morrowind/Oblivion are 3E
427-433; ESO is 2E 582 (about 700 years earlier). Every claim below carries its era, and where
eras could disagree the 4E 201 state is stated explicitly.

## 1. State of the faith in 4E 201

**Political frame (4E, in-game).** High Rock stayed in the Empire under Titus Mede II, sent
Legion reinforcements to Hammerfell in the Great War, and fought at the Battle of the Red Ring
(The Great War, Skyrim in-game book). At 4E 201 the Bretons have "good relations with the
Empire" and refuse the Stormcloaks' request for aid (Ulfric/Galmar dialogue, Skyrim). A Breton
at Skyrim's date is a citizen of a loyal Imperial province.

**The Divines are the mainstream.** Bretons transitioned to the Eight Divines under the
Alessian Empire, and the Eight are worshipped widely in mainstream Breton culture (Varieties
of Faith -- 3E in-game, with a 2E ESO copy; The Improved Emperor's Guide -- OOG print). The
institutional depth is best attested in Daggerfall (3E 405, in-game): each of the Eight has
its own Iliac Bay temple network -- Akatosh Chantry, Order of Arkay, House of Dibella, School
of Julianos, Temple of Kynareth, Benevolence of Mara, Temple of Stendarr, Resolution of
Zenithar -- and each temple fields its own templar knightly order as its militant arm
(Daggerfall faction data). Opinions differ on Breton piety: "not disposed to excessive
religion" (A History of Daggerfall, in-game) vs nearly as devout as Colovians (Improved
Emperor's Guide -- OOG). Era note: no 4E source depicts High Rock's temple institutions
directly, but nothing depicts their fall either; continuity is the safe read.

**Talos is a problem, not a ninth pillar.** Daggerfall's eight temple factions include no Talos
cult (3E 405, in-game), and after the White-Gold Concordat (4E 175) Talos worship is banned
empire-wide (The Great War; Thalmor dialogue, Skyrim -- both 4E in-game). In a province this
loyal to the Empire, public Talos worship at 4E 201 is contraband. The mod's Breton Talos offer
and champion boon are a deliberate design extension, not a lore-led slot; any Breton Talos copy
should carry the same suppressed/dissident texture the Imperial lane already models.

**Hero-god syncretism is alive.** Beyond the Eight, "Sheor, Phynaster, Y'ffre and Magnus have
significant cults in High Rock" (Varieties of Faith -- 3E in-game). Sheor survives at 4E 201 at
least as an oath in a Breton's mouth (Phinis Gestor's dialogue, Skyrim -- 4E in-game). The
elven-descended strand is a live inheritance, not an antiquarian one: worship of elven gods
persists in modern Breton culture, and Bretons who emphasize their elven blood are drawn to
Phynaster (Varieties of Faith: The Bretons -- 2E ESO copy of the 3E text).

**Witches, the Wyrd, and legible Daedra-trafficking.** The Wyrd covens -- female, animistic
nature-witches descended from the same Nedic root as the druids -- persist across eras: a dozen
or so covens seeded from High Rock (Witch Cults of Northern High Rock -- ESO, 2E), working
covens you can visit, quest for, and buy summonings from in Daggerfall (3E 405, in-game), and
Glenmoril witches physically present in 4E 201 Skyrim (Companions questline, in-game). Rural
High Rock holds its witches and medicine men in high regard (Pocket Guide 1st Ed., High Rock --
OOG print). The load-bearing fact for the mod: in Daggerfall a witch coven will summon a
Daedric Prince FOR you, as a service, for gold and reputation -- and the temples and Mages
Guild run summoning days of their own (Daggerfall, in-game). Daedra contact in Breton lands is
a known, priced, procedural transaction -- legible -- while remaining socially dangerous:
Daedra worship is spoken of as something to be cleansed, a Daggerfall count was ousted for
Meridia worship, and only Azura's Spirit Wardens earned official toleration, for their
usefulness (all ESO, 2E; the count via FLAVOR: Artifact Record: Opal Charm). Price known,
stigma real: that is the Breton Daedric posture in every era we can see.

**The druids are gone; the Wyrd remained.** The Druids of Galen left High Rock for the Systres
around 1E 330; by the Interregnum druids had been absent from High Rock for centuries (ESO
dialogue, 2E). ESO's rich Druidism (circles, Draoife, druid-knights) is SYSTRES material, 2E,
and must not be projected onto 4E High Rock wholesale. Mainland traces exist -- a few druids
stayed behind (ESO dialogue), and druids appear in Iliac Bay funerary imagery circa 3E 417
(Daggerfall art) -- but the 4E-alive carrier of Breton nature-faith is the Wyrd, plus Y'ffre's
named cult in High Rock (Varieties of Faith -- 3E). A Breton Green Way at 4E 201 is therefore a
revival/remnant fantasy, not membership in a standing druidic institution.

**The Direnni inheritance.** Clan Direnni ruled the proto-Bretons as a noble caste from the
Adamantine Tower, interbred with them, and gave the race its half-elven blood (The Bretons:
Mongrels or Paragons? -- ESO, 2E; Pocket Guide 1st/3rd Ed. -- OOG). A remnant persists on
Balfiera into the Fourth Era (The Infernal City, ca. 4E 48 -- OOG novel). For the mod the
Direnni matter as ancestry and as Phynaster's sponsoring memory, not as a living cult.

## 2. Pantheon

Backbone source: Varieties of Faith in the Empire (Brother Mikhael Karkuxor; 3E in-game, in
Morrowind/Oblivion) and its ESO sect copy Varieties of Faith: The Bretons (2E). The Breton list
is the Eight Divines plus four "additional deities with significant Breton cults": Magnus,
Y'ffre, Sheor, Phynaster. Daggerfall (3E 405) supplies the institutional texture per god.

**Akatosh (Dragon God of Time)** -- chief of the Eight; endurance, invincibility, everlasting
legitimacy (Varieties of Faith). Hailed by the Akatosh Chantry with the Order of the Hour as
its sword; patron deity of Wayrest and the Isle of Balfiera (Daggerfall faction data, 3E). In
Breton lore he is married to Mara (Varieties of Faith) -- a genuinely Breton wrinkle worth copy
use. Mod use: Knight's Road oath/order/streak god. Fit: strong.

**Mara (Mother-Goddess)** -- in High Rock specifically the Mother-Goddess, wed to Akatosh
(Varieties of Faith). Benevolence of Mara runs her temples; the Maran Knights defend them;
patron of Anticlere, Betony, and other regions; Appreciation Day is hers (Daggerfall, 3E).
Mod use: Knight's Road community/mercy, plus shared hearth rows. Fit: strong.

**Stendarr (God of Mercy)** -- "patron of magistrates, rulers, and knights errant"; compassion
and righteous rule (Varieties of Faith). Temple of Stendarr heals and trains; its knightly
order is the Crusaders (Daggerfall, 3E). At 4E 201 his live expression in Skyrim is the
Vigilants of Stendarr, founded after the Oblivion Crisis (Skyrim, in-game). The single
best-attested Knight's Road god, and the one whose 4E surface (Vigilants, Hall of the
Vigilant, Stendarr's Beacon) the engine can actually see. Fit: strong.

**Zenithar (God of Work and Commerce)** -- in High Rock "a far more cultivated god of
merchants, artisans, and the middle nobility"; the god "that will always win" (Varieties of
Faith). Resolution of Zenithar; Knights of Iron (Daggerfall, 3E). Fit: strong as a civic
Divine; correctly unlaned in the mod -- no knightly, occult, or druidic identity.

**Julianos (God of Wisdom and Logic)** -- god of literature, law, history, and contradiction,
and "the favorite deity of most Breton mages" (Varieties of Faith). His temples are Schools,
educational institutions credited with spreading the very word "magicka" (Daggerfall; King
Edward -- in-game); Knights Mentor are his order. Note the tension the mod already exploits:
lore-Julianos is the MAGES' Divine, so his double life in the mod (Knight's Road law/study
patron who also scores Hidden Art study rows in the player guide) is not drift, it is the god.

**Dibella (Goddess of Beauty)** -- popular; many cults (women, artists, erotic instruction)
(Varieties of Faith). House of Dibella and Order of the Lily administer her High Rock temples
(Daggerfall, 3E); a 4E surface exists via Markarth's temple. Fit: fine as roster breadth.

**Arkay (God of the Cycle of Life and Death)** -- burials and funeral rites; priests staunch
enemies of necromancy (Varieties of Faith). A Breton story makes Arkay a mortal who ascended
(Ark'ay The God -- in-game); Order of Arkay and its Knights of the Circle (Daggerfall, 3E).
Fit: strong for vigil/undead-duty rows.

**Kynareth (Goddess of Air)** -- patron of sailors and travelers; Betony is her holy island,
worth a war (Varieties of Faith; The Daggerfall Chronicles -- OOG for the war detail). Her
Temple's physicians were unrivaled (Daggerfall, 3E). Mod use: Knight's Road breadth, plus
proxy-only routing into the Green Way -- correct restraint: she is a Divine of air and
healing, not the covenant god.

**Magnus (Magus)** -- god of sorcery; withdrew from creation at the last second; what remains
of him is felt and controlled as magic; can possess powerful magicians and lend them power
(Varieties of Faith). A significant Breton cult (same); wicker-effigy rituals (FLAVOR: ESO
Glenumbra contraband); the Priory of the Golden Staff was a Breton Magnus sect with genuinely
heretical cosmology (Doombringer Celdina's Testament -- ESO, 2E); Imperials pointedly do NOT
worship him (ESO Q&A -- 2E), making Magnus a distinctly Breton-facing arcane god. Fit:
thin-but-defensible as the Hidden Art's most direct god -- the cult is real and Breton, but no
source paints Magnus as FORBIDDEN study; that coloring is the mod's own frame.

**Y'ffre / Jephre (God of the Forest)** -- the spirit of "the now"; became the first of the
Earth Bones, establishing the laws of nature (Varieties of Faith). Named a significant Breton
cult (same); "still revered by some Breton hunters and farmers" (Improved Emperor's Guide --
OOG print); the Beldama Wyrd worship him (Witch Cults of Northern High Rock -- ESO, 2E); the
druids' primary deity, the Green-King (Wyrd and Druid -- ESO, 2E). Fit: strong as the Green
Way's owner; see era note in section 1 on druids-vs-Wyrd.

**Phynaster (Guardian, Preserver, Wayfarer, Prowler)** -- hero-god of the Summerset Isles who
taught the Altmer to live another hundred years by shortening their stride; "patron deity and
teacher of the Direnni"; "often worshiped by those Breton mages who emphasize their Elven
blood" (Varieties of Faith: The Bretons -- the constituency line is in the ESO copy). His Ring
grants resistance to poison, magicka, and shock and is credited as the source of his longevity
(Tamrielic Lore -- in-game; the ring appears in Arena, Daggerfall, Morrowind). Sourced cult
practice -- pilgrimage passes as tests of faith, child-warding dolls, travelers' boot charms --
is all SUMMERSET, 2E (ESO dialogue and FLAVOR). An early-1E order of Breton mage-thieves read
him as the Prowler (Mylen Dechery's Journal -- ESO, 2E). Full assessment in section 5b.

**Sheor (the Bad Man)** -- source of all strife in High Rock; began as a god of crop failure;
modern theologians read him as a demonized Shor/Lorkhan born after the fall of Saarthal
(Varieties of Faith). Pre-demonization Manmer idols pair him lovingly with Mara (FLAVOR: ESO
Greymoor antiquities). Bretons still swear by him at 4E 201 (Phinis Gestor's dialogue, Skyrim
-- 4E in-game). No worship practice exists to build on; he is the pantheon's warning label.

**The four Hidden Art princes, as Bretons read them:**
- **Hircine** -- the strongest Breton-Daedric thread. Glenmoril Wyrd beast-form magic is
  attributed to knowledge of his Hunting Grounds (OOG: developer posts; corroborated by their
  in-game portfolio); druids honor him as the Elk of Arrows (FLAVOR: ESO Nighthunter's Cowl);
  in Daggerfall the Glenmoril Coven of High Rock summons Hircine on request -- an agent of the
  Blades paid them to (Daggerfall, 3E in-game); and Glenmoril witches operate in 4E 201 Skyrim
  (Companions questline, in-game). Era-solid at both ends.
- **Hermaeus Mora** -- Breton-specific attestation is effectively absent; the tie is an
  inference from Breton wizardly culture plus his strong race-agnostic 4E Skyrim surface
  (Discerning the Transmundane; Black Books -- in-game). Legible-to-a-witch is defensible;
  Breton-flavored is not sourced.
- **Nocturnal** -- the weakest of the four for Bretons. No High Rock worship thread surfaced;
  her 4E expression is the Nightingale compact (Skyrim, race-agnostic). The one Breton
  thief-cult found worships Phynaster-as-Prowler, not Nocturnal (Mylen Dechery's Journal,
  ESO). Defensible only via the generic coven-summoning interface.
- **Namira** -- moderate. UESP files her under the Glenmoril Wyrd's faith; her worship mass
  sits with the Reachfolk (ESO, 2E -- Reachmen are NOT Bretons, though the cultures bleed).
  At 4E 201 the Taste of Death coven at Markarth is led by Eola, a Breton (Skyrim, in-game).
  Thin-but-real at Skyrim's own date.

## 3. The mod's lanes

**The Knight's Road (chivalric Divines devotion).** History: Breton society is famous for its
knightly orders; the founding myths are chivalric liberation tales, and every Divine's temple
fields a templar order (Knightly Orders of High Rock -- ESO, 2E; Daggerfall faction data, 3E).
Breton knights espouse traditional chivalric codes and are High Rock's most prominent questers
(Tamrielic Lore; ESO). 4E continuity is indirect (no 4E source shows the orders), but Stendarr's
4E Vigilants give the lane a live Skyrim-facing institution. Player fantasy: the vow carried
into a province that rewards breaking it -- honor as a daily, expensive choice, with the
Divines' recognition as the payoff. Designer note: the lane is really Daggerfall's
temple-plus-templar culture walked by one person.

**The Hidden Art (legible occult scholarship/witchcraft).** History: magic permeates Breton
society from serf to king (OOG: developer material; corroborated by PGE1's child-testing and
court-wizard culture -- OOG print); witch-kings once ruled High Rock (Corpse Preparation --
in-game; Redguard dialogue, 3E); covens summon Princes as a priced service while polite
society keeps its distance (Daggerfall, 3E in-game). Breton doctrine even supplies the moral
frame the mod's WitchcraftExposure needs: black magic is a matter of intent, not effect (The
Light and the Dark -- in-game). Player fantasy: the double life -- private mastery of things
your neighbors fear, with exposure as the currency; you are not damned for the practice, only
priced for being seen. Designer note: this is the most lore-faithful lane in the race; the
Daggerfall coven interface IS this lane rendered as 3E gameplay.

**The Green Way (Y'ffre/Wyrd druidic lane).** History: the Nedic ancestors followed Y'ffre's
True Way; the druids left for the Systres in 1E 330 and the Wyrd stayed to ward High Rock's
forests (Wyrd and Druid; ESO dialogue -- 2E); Y'ffre keeps a named cult in High Rock and Breton
hunters and farmers still revere Jephre (Varieties of Faith -- 3E; Improved Emperor's Guide --
OOG). Player fantasy: hearing the oldest Breton faith under the newer ones -- covenant, not
conquest; the forest taught to recognize you. Designer note: at 4E 201 this is a remnant/revival
walk (Wyrd texture, standing stones, outdoor covenant), not membership in ESO's druid circles;
copy should avoid implying a standing mainland druidic order.

**The retired Tradition substrate.** PDV_Bless_Breton_Tradition_T1/T2, the ancestor spine
(PDV_Substrate_BretonAncestor and its three Spine slots), and PDV_Bless_Breton_PatronChampion
are save-compatibility/force-removal records only (spec status 2026-07-13; registry contract in
the spec's substrateBoons block). They are not a fourth lane and take no new content. Any doc
that presents "Tradition" as a live reward family is describing the pre-2026-07 model.

## 4. God-to-lane associations, with evidence

Graded per the Redguard convention: lore-strong vs thin-but-defensible.

- **Stendarr -> Knight's Road: lore-strong.** Knights errant are literally in his portfolio
  (Varieties of Faith); Crusaders/Temple of Stendarr (Daggerfall, 3E); Vigilants (Skyrim, 4E).
- **Akatosh -> Knight's Road: lore-strong.** Chantry + Order of the Hour (Daggerfall);
  oath/endurance/legitimacy portfolio (Varieties of Faith); wed to Mara in Breton telling.
- **Mara -> Knight's Road (+hearth rows): lore-strong.** Mother-Goddess of High Rock;
  Benevolence + Maran Knights (Daggerfall).
- **Arkay -> Knight's Road vigil/undead duty: lore-strong.** Anti-necromancy priesthood
  (Varieties of Faith); Order of Arkay + Knights of the Circle (Daggerfall).
- **Julianos -> Knight's Road law/study AND Hidden Art study rows: lore-strong for the dual
  role.** "Favorite deity of most Breton mages" (Varieties of Faith) -- the lore god leans
  arcane; keeping his lawful face in Knight's Road and his study appetite feeding Hidden Art
  rows is the honest split, not a contradiction.
- **Kynareth, Dibella, Zenithar -> Knight's Road breadth: lore-adequate.** Full temple
  institutions each (Daggerfall); no knightly-specific identity, which is why they are breadth
  rather than lane leads. Kynareth's Green Way PROXY-ONLY routing is correct restraint.
- **Talos -> Knight's Road offer: lore-thin and era-loaded.** No Daggerfall temple (3E);
  banned worship at 4E 201 in a pro-Imperial province (The Great War, Skyrim). Ship only with
  dissident/suppressed texture.
- **Magnus -> Hidden Art lead: thin-but-defensible.** Significant Breton cult (Varieties of
  Faith), ritual practice (ESO FLAVOR), heretical Breton sect precedent (Priory of the Golden
  Staff, ESO) -- but the "forbidden" coloring is the mod's, not the sources'.
- **Y'ffre -> Green Way: lore-strong.** Cult in High Rock (Varieties of Faith); Beldama Wyrd
  worship (Witch Cults of Northern High Rock, ESO); druidic primary deity (Wyrd and Druid).
- **Phynaster -> Green Way: thin, and pointed the other way.** His Breton constituency is
  MAGES who emphasize elven blood (Varieties of Faith: The Bretons); his walking/pilgrimage
  texture is Summerset-attested (ESO, 2E). Longevity/stride/heritage supports the mod's
  pilgrimage-endurance spine as texture, but by the sources he is closer kin to the Hidden
  Art's heritage-proud wizards than to the nature covenant. See 5b.
- **Hircine -> Hidden Art (+Green Way fork): lore-strong.** Glenmoril summoning (Daggerfall,
  3E in-game); Wyrd beast-magic; Elk of Arrows; 4E Skyrim presence. The Druidic Trial fork
  (beast serves the Green vs Hircine's gift) mirrors a real split: druids honor him as Elk of
  Arrows while the Glenmoril lean Daedric -- the two readings exist in the sources.
- **Hermaeus Mora -> Hidden Art: thin-but-defensible.** No Breton thread; carried by the
  generic witch-scholar frame and a strong race-agnostic 4E surface.
- **Nocturnal -> Hidden Art: thin.** No Breton thread found; carried entirely by the coven
  interface and Skyrim's own Nightingale content.
- **Namira -> Hidden Art: thin-but-defensible.** Glenmoril-faith filing; a Breton runs her
  Skyrim cult at 4E 201 (Eola, in-game).

## 5. Options analysis (contested slots)

Ground truth (2026-08-27 drift survey; trust over any doc): code ships GreenWay/HiddenArt/
KnightsRoad reward families T1-T3; Tradition_T1/T2 retained only for save compatibility;
Champion is a patron property (no per-deity champion RECORDS in the manifest's promised
PDV_Bless_Breton_<Deity>_T3 shape); 11 offer records matching the guard at
tools/pdv_formal_offer_check.mjs:48-59 (Akatosh, Arkay, Dibella, Julianos, Kynareth, Magnus,
Mara, Stendarr, Talos, Yffre, Zenithar). No PDV_Deity_Phynaster.psc exists.

### 5a. Champion model: eleven per-deity records vs patron-property on three lane families

The manifest (race-sheets/PDV_RaceContent_Manifest.md:1576, :1608-1617) promises per-deity T3
blessing records for a ten-god roster including Phynaster and the four princes. The code and
the reward spec ship the other model: each tradition's family carries T1/T2/Champion, and the
Champion boon is a property of the active patron (spec notes, 2026-07-13) -- with the lane
capstones (Knight's Bulwark for Stendarr, Green Way for Y'ffre, Hidden Art for the integrated
Prince) plus mapped per-patron boons for the non-capstone Aedrics and Magnus, mirroring the
canonical per-deity T3 magnitudes verbatim (Imperial T3s; the Altmer T3 for Magnus).
PDV_TargetEndStates_1.0.md:796-798 describes three tradition-level Champions, agreeing with
the code against the manifest.

- **(a1) Keep the shipped patron-property model; delete the manifest's phantom rows.**
  Lore: the design's own thesis -- "the tradition comes first, the gods give it shape" -- is
  the sourced Breton shape: identity organizes around institutions (temple, order, coven,
  covenant) with the god as that institution's face (section 3). A tradition-tier ladder
  crowned by the patron's boon expresses exactly that. Mechanics: zero build; the deletion is
  documentation. Cost: manifest surgery with tombstones. Risk: none found -- the player guide
  already teaches this model.
- **(a2) Build the manifest's per-deity T3 ladders.** Lore: would recast Breton faith as
  eleven parallel single-god ladders -- the Imperial shape, which the Breton design exists to
  contrast. Mechanics: ~10 new reward families, offer/suppression rework, budget-ledger
  violation (two-family ceiling), and it contradicts the locked spec. No lore or design pull.
- **(a3) Bespoke champion PRESENTATION for select deities on top of (a1).** The three lane
  capstones already give Stendarr, Y'ffre, and the Prince bespoke presentation. The one
  candidate with lore texture to spare is Akatosh (wed to Mara in Breton telling; the streak
  mechanic is already his). This is copy-only work in the Champion Survey text if wanted.

**RECOMMENDATION (not a decision): (a1)**, with (a3) available as optional copy polish. The
lore and the mod's own design language both support tradition-first with the champion as the
patron's crowning property; the manifest's eleven rows are a drafting artifact of the older
per-deity frame.

### 5b. Phynaster: proof-gated patron vs hero-texture

Current state: proof-gated in RaceDesign_Breton:143-149 (gate restated with owner criteria in
the reward spec's phynasterAcceptanceGate); no deity script; yet manifest rows ship him as a
champion-tier entry (:1608-1617 table, :2057 roster row) -- phantom.

Lore strength, honestly weighed:
- FOR focusability: he is a real god of the attested Breton pantheon with a NAMED Breton
  constituency ("Breton mages who emphasize their Elven blood" -- Varieties of Faith: The
  Bretons) and a named historical sponsor (patron/teacher of the Direnni). His portfolio
  (longevity, wards, safe passage, the disciplined stride) is distinctive and un-owned by any
  other Breton god, and his artifact's resist-poison/magicka/shock profile (Tamrielic Lore,
  3E) dovetails uncannily with the mod's resist-magic capstone. Among PDV's proof-gated gods
  he is unusually well-anchored -- stronger than Tava's sect claim was for Redguard.
- AGAINST: every worship PRACTICE we can source (pilgrimage passes, warding dolls, boot
  charms) is Summerset cult practice, 2E; Breton practice is unattested beyond "worshiped by
  mages." His constituency is arcane/heritage, not druidic -- the Green Way placement rests on
  walking/endurance texture, and the design doc already concedes he is "not a second nature
  god." And the signal problem is the familiar one: pilgrimage/travel is this codebase's
  weakest detection class (the Redguard baseline's Tava finding, verbatim), while his other
  natural family -- "appreciation of elven heritage" -- is curated-list work with farm risk.
- **(b1) Keep him flavor/support; gate stays closed for 2.0.** Zero build; the gate's
  failure outcome is already specced. The manifest rows are deleted as phantoms either way.
- **(b2) Run the acceptance gate as written** (3 positive families: pilgrimage/endurance,
  elven heritage/longevity, Direnni memory; 1 dislike family; distinct copy; no-overlap with
  Y'ffre). Lore can fill the copy; the hard part is honest Skyrim-detectable sources for
  families 1 and 3. If the gate is ever run, note the lore would better support him attached
  to heritage-proud MAGE conduct than to Green Way nature conduct -- which risks colliding
  with the tradition-purity of the lanes.
- **(b3) Recast him as Hidden Art heritage texture** (no focus). Matches the sources' mage
  constituency; costs the Green Way its only second name, which the design tolerated anyway.

**RECOMMENDATION (not a decision): (b1)** for the current wave -- he is the rare proof-gated
god who genuinely could pass a future gate, so keep the gate rather than deleting the concept;
delete only the phantom champion rows. Note for the ruling: the gate text at RaceDesign:143-147
is undated and pending-owner; if (b1) is ratified it should acquire a date and an owner line.

### 5c. Daedric legibility: is the Hidden Art waiver correct and sufficient?

Current mechanic (acceptance criteria, RaceDesign:307): Hidden Art princes are reached via the
universal Daedric system; the matrix stance is Legible for Mora/Hircine/Nocturnal/Namira; for
a Breton on the Hidden Art the global Prince price and Taboo stigma do NOT stack -- Witchcraft
Exposure plus authored Vigilant pressure is the sole cost. The manifest's Breton champion rows
for the four princes (:1608-1617) are phantoms; the princes correctly have no Breton offer
records.

- Lore verdict on the waiver itself: **correct, and precisely shaped.** The Daggerfall coven
  interface is the canon proof that Breton culture prices Daedra contact in gold, reputation,
  and social distance rather than in cosmic penalty -- the transaction is known and survivable
  (Daggerfall, 3E in-game), while the stigma is real (2E cleansing rhetoric; the ousted
  Meridia-worshipping count). "Price+stigma waived, exposure is the cost" is that exact
  cultural posture translated to mechanics. No source supports Bretons paying LESS social cost
  than the mod charges, and none supports a second, metaphysical cost on top.
- Is it sufficient, or does lore support more? Three candidate extensions:
  - **(c1) Keep as-is.** The waiver plus WitchcraftExposure already models everything the
    sources attest. Recommended baseline.
  - **(c2) Broaden the legible set.** Daggerfall covens summon ANY prince (random, in-game),
    and 2E High Rock tolerated Azura's Spirit Wardens -- so lore could defend legibility
    beyond the four. But the four chosen are the ones with actual Breton worship threads
    (uneven as they are: Hircine strong, Namira moderate, Mora/Nocturnal thin), and widening
    the matrix multiplies Phase 20C surface for no new fantasy. Defensible, not compelled.
  - **(c3) Coven-broker presentation.** The most lore-flavored upgrade would be presentation
    only: Hidden Art copy that frames Daedric contact through the witch-coven interface (the
